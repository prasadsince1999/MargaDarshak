Privacy Policy

Margadarshak — Career Guidance for Indian Students

Effective Date: May 26, 2026  |  Last Updated: May 26, 2026

Margadarshak ("we", "our", "the app") is a career guidance application designed for Indian students and their parents. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal data responsibly, in compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and applicable Google Play policies.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information during onboarding to personalise your career guidance experience:

  • Name — to personalise the app experience
  • Role — Student or Parent
  • Education stage & details — class, board, stream, discipline
  • Location — state and city (for state-specific admission rules and scholarships)
  • Eligibility factors — reservation category, income bracket, PwD status (for eligibility checks)
  • Interests & target exams — to recommend relevant career paths
  • Dream goal & backup preference — to generate personalised roadmaps
We do NOT collect: Aadhaar numbers, bank details, passwords, precise GPS location, photos, contacts, call logs, or any biometric data.

2. How We Store Your Data

All personal data is currently stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences. Your data is not transmitted to any external server, cloud service, or third party.

In future versions, if we introduce cloud-based features (such as account sync or cloud backup), we will update this policy before enabling those features, and notify you through the app.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to:

  • Provide stage-aware career guidance tailored to your profile
  • Check eligibility for courses, exams, and scholarships
  • Simulate the impact of subject or stream changes
  • Show relevant career roadmaps and exam information
  • Enable Parent Mode features (Family Bridge pairing)

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or selling to third parties.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or transfer your personal data to any third party. Your data stays on your device.

5. User-Generated Content

The Student Voice feature allows users to submit feedback about institutions and courses. Survey responses may include opinions and ratings. These submissions are:

  • Stored locally in the current version
  • Subject to moderation when cloud features are introduced
  • Not linked to your personal identity unless you choose to identify yourself

6. Children's Privacy

Margadarshak is designed for students from Class 9 onwards (typically age 14+). For users under 18:

  • Sensitive features may require guardian awareness or consent
  • Assessment results are not publicly exposed without consent
  • The Parent Mode (Family Bridge) allows guardians to participate in the guidance process

We follow the principle of data minimisation — we collect only what is necessary to provide career guidance.

7. Your Rights

Under the DPDP Act 2023, you have the right to:

  • Access — View all data the app holds about you (visible in your Profile)
  • Correction — Update your information at any time through the app's Settings
  • Erasure — Delete all your personal data from the app. You can do this by clearing app data from your device settings, or using the in-app data deletion option.
  • Withdraw consent — Stop using the app at any time; your data remains local and can be deleted

8. Data Security

  • All data is stored locally on-device in the app's private storage
  • Any network communication (when introduced) will use HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • We do not store data on external servers in the current version

9. Data Retention

Your data is retained on your device for as long as the app is installed. Uninstalling the app or clearing app data will permanently delete all stored information. There is no server-side backup to recover data after deletion.

10. Third-Party Services

The current version of Margadarshak does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. If future versions introduce third-party services (e.g., Firebase, analytics), this policy will be updated accordingly.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be communicated through in-app notifications.
