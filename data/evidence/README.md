# Evidence cards

[`evidence-cards.json`](evidence-cards.json) holds 78 public accounts of Indian students hurt by a hidden rule, deadline, document problem or bad advice. It is the open-data version of the [Mārgadarshak evidence library](https://ksmxtech.com/margadarshak/evidence-library/), after its 20 October 2026 source cleanup.

| Source type | Cards |
|---|---|
| Reddit | 37 |
| Court case | 13 |
| News media | 9 |
| YouTube | 8 |
| Forum | 7 |
| Quora | 4 |

Each card has:
- `situation` and `consequence`: our summary, in our words
- `appFeature`: the app feature it motivates, by its current name
- `websiteLabel`: the older name shown on the website
- `sourceUrl`: the original post, article or judgment

**What these cards are, and what they are not:**
- They show that a kind of problem happens and why a feature exists. They are **not** a count of how often it happens, and they don't prove an app would have prevented the outcome.
- Every card is marked `Review pending`. A per-card review of sources and summaries is ongoing.
- The original words belong to their authors. We link to them rather than copy them, and they are not covered by this repository's licence.
- One website card (`story-051`) has no source link and is held back until a source is re-linked.
