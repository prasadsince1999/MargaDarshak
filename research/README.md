# Research

- [**Show each stage the gates it faces**](indian-student-problems-by-stage.md) (20 October 2026). This is a stage-by-stage study of problems Indian students actually report. It covers Class 10, +2, Diploma, ITI, Bachelor's, Master's and droppers, and draws on court judgments, official reports, news and public student questions. It ends with a stage × feature matrix and ranked feature ideas.
- [`notes/`](notes/): the nine sourced research notes behind the report, one per stage, plus court cases and an audit of the earlier evidence library.

Each claim carries an evidence label: official rule/data, court judgment, news report, survey, single anecdote or coaching-site claim. Rules quoted here came partly through secondary sources. The **rule cards** in [`data/rule-cards/`](../data/rule-cards/) are the checked versions, and they win where the two differ.
