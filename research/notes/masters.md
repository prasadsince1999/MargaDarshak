# Indian Master's / Postgraduate Students (MSc, MA, MCom, MTech, MBA, MCA): problems during the course and at the next decision

Research date: 20 October 2026. India only. Sources are 2023–2026 unless flagged. Secondary aggregators (Careers360, PW, Oliveboard, Biotecnika) are used where official PDFs were not retrievable; each such point is marked "verify against the official bulletin".

Stage codes: **S** = studying the Master's · **F** = final year · **C** = completed.
App-fit codes: **Yes / Partly / No**. Feature names are the existing Mārgadarshak set: Compare Paths, What If, Eligibility, Exam Stack, Cost Loan & ROI Calculator, Document Check, Foundation Check, Explore, My Plan, Parent Mode, or "new feature needed".

## Q1. Next-step decisions: PhD vs job vs teaching; NET/JRF/CSIR NET rules; NET-for-PhD; state SET; GATE for PSUs; MBA placements

### Takeaway
After a Master's, the student faces a decision with many interacting rules. These include the 55%/50% marks floor, a provisional "result-awaited" window of one or two years, a JRF age cap of 30 with relaxations, three NET result categories that unlock different things, and NET scores for PhD admission that stay valid for only one year. State SET is valid only in its own state, and a GATE score for PSU jobs is valid for one year. This is a strong fit for a rules-based Eligibility and Exam Stack layer. The app cannot fix the scarcity: about 1,932 JRFs went to 6,988 CSIR NET qualifiers in December 2023.

### Cited Findings

**UGC NET eligibility (rules an app can encode)**
- Marks: General, Unreserved and General-EWS candidates need at least 55% in the Master's, with no rounding off. OBC-NCL, SC, ST, PwD and third-gender candidates need 50%. Candidates who finished their Master's by 19 Sept 1991 get a 5% relaxation. — [PW (June 2026 criteria summary)](https://www.pw.live/ugc-net/exams/ugc-net-eligibility); also [Oliveboard](https://www.oliveboard.in/blog/ugc-net-eligibility-criteria/). Verify against the bulletin.
- Final-year and result-awaited candidates may apply provisionally. For JRF or Assistant Professor, they must finish the Master's with the required percentage within **two years** of the NET result. For "PhD admission only", the limit is **one year**. — [PW](https://www.pw.live/ugc-net/exams/ugc-net-eligibility)
- JRF age: at most 30 years, measured on a cut-off date (1 June 2026 for the June 2026 session). There is no upper age limit for Assistant Professor. — [PW](https://www.pw.live/ugc-net/exams/ugc-net-eligibility); [Careers360](https://competition.careers360.com/articles/ugc-net-eligibility-criteria/amp). Secondary sources give **different cut-off dates for different sessions** (1 June vs 1 December), so the date must come from each session's bulletin. — [WebSearch synthesis of Careers360/PW/Oliveboard; conflicting dates](https://www.pw.live/ugc-net/exams/ugc-net-december-2025)
- JRF age relaxations: up to 5 years for OBC-NCL, SC, ST, PwD, third gender and women. Research experience and armed-forces service also count, subject to conditions. LLM holders get 3 years. Total relaxation is capped at 5 years. — [PW](https://www.pw.live/ugc-net/exams/ugc-net-eligibility)
- Four-year bachelor's holders with 75% (5% relaxation for some categories) can take NET for JRF and PhD, **but not for Assistant Professor**. — [PW](https://www.pw.live/ugc-net/exams/ugc-net-eligibility)

**CSIR NET (science) eligibility**
- JRF age cap is 30 as on 1 July 2025, with 5 years' relaxation for SC/ST, third gender, PwD and women and 3 years for OBC-NCL. There is no age limit for Lectureship/NET. **One source (Vedprep) says 28**, which conflicts with the others. — [Oliveboard](https://www.oliveboard.in/blog/csir-net-eligibility-criteria); [Sciastra](https://www.sciastra.com/blogs/csir-net-eligibility-2025-age-limit-and-educational-qualification); conflicting: [Vedprep](https://vedprep.com/exams/csir-net/2025/)
- Marks floor: 55% for General/EWS/**OBC** and 50% for SC/ST/PwD, as reported by aggregators. This differs from UGC NET, where OBC-NCL gets 50%. — [Sciastra](https://www.sciastra.com/blogs/csir-net-eligibility-2025-age-limit-and-educational-qualification). Treat the OBC difference as unverified until checked in the CSIR NET bulletin.
- Result-awaited candidates must complete the degree within two years. Bachelor's-only candidates can get JRF only. — [Oliveboard](https://www.oliveboard.in/blog/csir-net-eligibility-criteria)

**Fellowship amounts**
- JRF is Rs 37,000/month and SRF Rs 42,000/month, effective 1 Jan 2023 (DST June 2023, UGC 572nd meeting Oct 2023, CSIR Oct 2023). CSIR JRF was previously Rs 31,000/35,000. — [Careers360 – DST](https://news.careers360.com/dst-hikes-jrf-srf-phd-fellowship-stipends-mixed-reactions-from-phd-scholars-research/amp); [Careers360 – UGC](https://news.careers360.com/ugc-revises-fellowship-amounts-for-jrf-srf-post-doctoral-other-schemes/amp); [Careers360 – CSIR](https://news.careers360.com/csir-hikes-jrf-srf-other-research-fellowship-stipends-csirhrdg-res-in/amp)
- The non-NET fellowship is **Rs 8,000/month, unchanged since 2012** (it was Rs 5,000 in 2006). In 2022-23, 15,397 PhD scholars in 40 central universities received it. — [Careers360, 27 Mar 2024](https://news.careers360.com/research-ugc-csir-net-exam-jrf-non-fellowship-phd-rs-8000-budget-delay-stipend-jnu-delhi-university-du-sc-st-obc-nep-2020/amp)
- The ICHR JRF stipend (Rs 17,600, as stated by ABVP in Feb 2024) is far below the UGC JRF. Funder-specific amounts differ. — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/abvp-demands-hike-ichr-jrf-fellowship-asks-ugc-extend-doctoral-fellowship-deadline)

**JRF scarcity (December 2023 cycle)**
- UGC NET Dec 2023: 9,45,872 registered, 6,95,928 appeared, 58,794 qualified and 5,032 got both Assistant Professor and JRF. CSIR NET Dec 2023: 6,988 qualified and 1,932 got JRF. — [Careers360, Mar 2024](https://news.careers360.com/research-ugc-csir-net-exam-jrf-non-fellowship-phd-rs-8000-budget-delay-stipend-jnu-delhi-university-du-sc-st-obc-nep-2020/amp)

**NET scores for PhD admission (UGC, March 2024)**
- The UGC 578th meeting (13 Mar 2024) allowed NET scores to replace university PhD entrance tests from 2024-25. — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/ugc-net-scores-can-be-used-for-phd-admissions-from-2024-25-session-decides-commission/amp)
- From June 2024, NET results come in three categories. Category 1 gives JRF, Assistant Professor and PhD. Category 2 gives Assistant Professor and PhD without JRF. Category 3 gives PhD admission only. Categories 2 and 3 are admitted on 70% NET score and 30% interview, and their score is **valid for one year** for PhD admission. Category 1 follows the PhD Regulations 2022, which in practice means an interview. — [Biotecnika, citing UGC notice 27.03.2024](https://www.biotecnika.org/2024/03/net-score-for-phd-admissions-2024-25-ugc-notice/); [ILI notice](https://ili.ac.in/details.php?scatid=29)
- Universities can still hold their own entrance tests in subjects with no NET paper. — [Biotecnika](https://www.biotecnika.org/2024/03/net-score-for-phd-admissions-2024-25-ugc-notice/)

**Teaching route: Assistant Professor eligibility**
- From 1 July 2023, NET/SET/SLET is the minimum qualification for direct recruitment as Assistant Professor in all HEIs, and a PhD is optional. A PhD is still needed for promotion to Associate Professor, and institutions may set higher shortlisting criteria. UGC later clarified that PhD holders also remain eligible. — [Biotecnika](https://www.biotecnika.org/2023/07/net-set-slet-mandatory-for-assistant-professor-post-phd-optional-from-this-date); [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/ugc-move-on-assistant-professor-eligibility-draws-mixed-reactions-commission-clarifies-phd-eligible/amp)
- State SET is **valid only in the conducting state**. For example, MH-SET certificates are valid in Maharashtra and Goa only, while NET is valid nationally. — [Pathfinder Academy](https://pathfinderacademy.in/state-eligibility-test.html); [MH-SET site](https://setexam.unipune.ac.in/)

**GATE → PSU (MTech / engineering Master's)**
- Many PSUs use GATE scores, typically as GATE score + GD/GT + interview. Examples include BHEL, ONGC, NTPC, PGCIL, IOCL, GAIL, NHPC, NPCIL and SAIL. The list changes every year and the official list is "not exhaustive". — [GATE 2026 IITG opportunities page](https://gate2026.iitg.ac.in/opportunities.html); [PW](https://www.pw.live/gate/exams/can-you-get-a-psu-job-with-your-gate-2026-rank)
- A GATE score is valid for **three years for MTech admission but only one year for PSU jobs**. — [PW](https://www.pw.live/gate/exams/gate-score-validity)

**MBA placement realities**
- AICTE data, as reported by ThePrint: 1,20,946 MBA/PGDM students were placed in 2021-22, a five-year high. ThePrint gives placement as 59% (2017-18), 61.8% (2018-19) and 83% (2021-22) **of graduating students**. AICTE's institute-level figures instead use students enrolled in the first year. — [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/education/management-placements-hit-a-5-year-high-in-2021-22-salaries-are-improving-too/1414562/)
- Management intake in 2022-23 was 4,22,628 approved seats with 2,85,367 enrolled, so **67.5% of seats were filled**. — [Careers360 (AICTE data)](https://news.careers360.com/aicte-management-mba-mca-pgdm-pharmacy-hotel-management-courses-admission-seats-enrollments-portal-data/amp)
- The available tier-2 placement and salary figures are ranges from blogs and Q&A, with no methodology (for example "60–90% placement, Rs 8–15 LPA"). They should not be used as facts. — [Careers360 Q&A](https://www.careers360.com/question-what-are-the-placement-statistics-for-the-mba-program); [PaGaLGuY](https://www.pagalguy.com/articles/mba-placements-2025-salary-trends-highest-packages-and-sector-insights)

### Inferences
- Combining the NET category, the result-awaited window (1 vs 2 years), JRF age with stacked relaxations (capped at 5), score validity (1 year for PhD), SET's home state and GATE validity (1 year for PSU vs 3 for MTech) gives a set of deterministic rules. That suits an **Eligibility + Exam Stack** engine, where the same inputs give the same answer. Every rule needs a dated source and the caveat "verify in this session's bulletin".
- A Category 3 qualifier may assume NET makes them eligible to teach, but Category 3 is PhD-only. Showing what each category unlocks is a high-value, low-cost feature.
- MBA placement claims should be shown as institute-reported figures with their denominator (enrolled vs graduated). The app should not rank colleges on placement.

### Gaps
- I could not retrieve the official NTA UGC NET or CSIR NET bulletin PDFs directly. The age cut-off date, the CSIR OBC marks floor and the exact category definitions need checking against ugcnet.nta.ac.in and csirnet.nta.ac.in for the session concerned.
- I found no official national MBA placement data for 2023-24 onward, and no reliable tier-2 or tier-3 placement data.
- I found no complete, current list of UGC-accredited state SETs. Odisha's status needs checking specifically: no source found in this pass.
- I found no source on MCA-specific or MCom-specific next-step outcomes.

## Q2. Problems during and after the PG course: low-value or unrecognised programmes, UGC-DEB online/distance recognition, overqualification, fellowship delays, supervisors, employability gap, the June 2024 NET cancellation

### Takeaway
The worst harms come from things the student cannot see when choosing. A programme or online mode may not be recognised. The JRF may be paid months late. The supervisor may effectively hold a veto. A national exam may be cancelled after it is sat. Postgraduates face 12.4% unemployment (PLFS 2023-24), close to graduates' 13%. An app can help with the visibility problems (recognition checks, scenario planning, backup plans). It cannot fix disbursement, supervision or exam integrity.

### Inferences
- MA students are the largest PG group, and they face the thinnest job routes outside teaching, NET and government exams. If the app supports PG, the MA/humanities route should be built first.
- Recognition (#1, #2) is the problem the app is most clearly placed to solve. The data is public and binary, and the harm (an invalid degree) is permanent. It also fits the studio's no-simulation rule: the app shows the official list and its date, and does not certify the institution.
- Fellowship delay (#9) and NET cancellation (#11) are best handled as risk scenarios in What If and Exam Stack. Promising mitigation would be dishonest.
- **Features that do NOT fit PG students (or fit only with major rework):**
  - **Foundation Check in its school form** (subject-foundation or aptitude checks for stream choice) is not relevant. Repurposing it for PG means checking research or skill profile gaps (publications, teaching experience, tools), which is effectively a new feature.
  - **Parent Mode as currently framed**, where a parent edits or steers a minor's profile. PG students are adults, typically 21–30. Parent involvement is mainly financial (funding a PhD on Rs 8,000, or an MBA loan) and emotional ("when will you earn?"). A read-only "explain my choice to family" share view fits; parental control of the profile does not, and the minor-consent logic does not apply.
  - **Stream and subject selection, Class 10/12 board-centred flows, and school-level scholarship discovery** (NMMSS and similar) do not apply. PG needs PG-specific schemes instead (JRF, non-NET fellowship, state PG scholarships, ICHR/ICSSR fellowships).
  - **UG entrance-exam content in Exam Stack** (JEE/NEET/CUET-UG) is irrelevant except as coaching-faculty job context. The PG Exam Stack is NET, CSIR NET, SET, GATE, CAT/MAT, UPSC/SSC/state PSC, bank PO and CA/CMA.
  - **Explore's course catalogue for "which degree next"** is weaker at PG. After a Master's, the realistic "next" is a PhD, a job, an exam or a professional certificate, not another degree. Explore needs a jobs-and-roles view by Master's subject.

### Gaps
- I found no PG-specific, India-wide survey on employability by subject (MSc vs MA vs MCom). The India Skills Report or AISHE outcome data would be needed. No sourced statistic is given here.
- There is no national data on how often fellowship delays happen or how long they last. Only individual cases (Coimbatore 2025, JNU 2024) were found.
- I found no national dropout data for PhDs or for supervisor-change frequency.
- I found no reliable source on whether the NET June 2024 cancellation caused people to miss JRF age cut-offs or PhD admission deadlines. That is a plausible effect but undocumented here.
- I did not check MCA/MCom-specific recognition issues (for example AICTE approval for MCA) in this pass.

### Inferences
- The question types map directly onto app features. "Am I eligible?" maps to Eligibility. "What can I do after X?" maps to Explore and Compare Paths. "How much is the stipend, and when is it paid?" maps to the Cost Loan & ROI Calculator. "NET or job/coaching?" maps to Compare Paths and What If.
- Stale stipend figures still circulating online support the app's rule of dated, source-linked figures.
- Many Careers360 Q&A questions are undated and probably older than 2020. They show recurring question shapes, but not how often each is asked now.

### Gaps
- No r/Indian_Academia or Quora phrasings were collected because the tools returned no Reddit or Quora pages. A manual pass on Reddit is recommended (searches such as "NET or job", "JRF age limit", "UGC-DEB valid", "non-NET PhD stipend", "MBA tier 3 worth it").
- There are no MBA, MCA or MCom question phrasings in this set. Every phrasing collected is MSc or JRF related.
