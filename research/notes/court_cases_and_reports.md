# Indian court cases and official reports: students who lost, or nearly lost, admissions, seats, jobs, scholarships or exam eligibility over information or process problems

Researched 20 October 2026. India only. Most items are from 2015–2026; older items are flagged. Search-engine summaries were cross-checked across at least two outlets where possible. Where only secondary or coaching-site coverage was found, that is stated. **No case name, date or holding below was added from memory without a source URL.**

**Honesty limit, applies to every "preventable" mapping:** Mārgadarshak can prompt self-checks (for example "does the name on your Class 10 certificate match Aadhaar exactly?"), show dated deadlines and link to official lists. It cannot verify a document, certify eligibility, confirm an institution's current approval or guarantee a refund. Every feature mapping below means "could have prompted the student to act earlier". It does not mean "would have guaranteed the outcome".

Feature keys: **Document Check**, **Eligibility**, **My Plan** (dated deadlines/reminders), **Explore** (recognised-institution info), **Cost Loan & ROI Calculator** (fee/refund), **Exam Stack**, **Parent Mode**, or **New feature needed**.

## 1. Name / date-of-birth mismatch across Class 10 certificate, Aadhaar and other documents

### Takeaway
The Supreme Court has held that board certificates must be correctable to match a student's identity documents. Recruitment and admission bodies still reject candidates over spelling or date mismatches, and some High Courts say Aadhaar is not proof of age. A mismatch found at the verification stage is the costly failure. A self-check months before verification is cheap.

### Cited Findings
- **Jigya Yadav (Minor) v. CBSE, Supreme Court, 3 June 2021 (Civil Appeal 3905 of 2011, batch of 22 matters).** Stage: Class 10 / +2 (board certificates), with downstream effect on every later stage. The student sought correction of her father's name on CBSE marksheets ("Hari Singh Yadav" recorded instead of "Hari Singh"). The Delhi High Court had upheld CBSE's refusal under Bye-law 69.1 (2007). The Supreme Court heard 22 petitions on correction or change of name, surname or date of birth of candidates or parents. Reports say it found the bye-law restrictions on changes unreasonable and issued guidelines, calling name "an intrinsic element of identity" and noting that Aadhaar and passport details can be changed. Preventable: **partly** (an early mismatch check gives time to file corrections before admission or recruitment deadlines). Features: **Document Check**, **Parent Mode** (parents' names are on the certificate). — [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/top-stories/cbse-certificates-supreme-court-calls-for-different-approach-in-time-limit-for-changescorrections-175180); [CBSE-hosted judgment PDF](https://www.cbse.gov.in/cbsenew/judgements/A10.pdf); [iPleaders analysis](https://blog.ipleaders.in/jigna-yadav-thru-her-father-cbse-2021-case-analysis/). *Conflict: one aggregator (LawText) summarises the ruling as upholding the bye-laws but requiring flexible application. Read the judgment text before quoting the exact guidelines.*
- **Delhi High Court, Indian Coast Guard Navik recruitment (reported 16 November 2024).** Stage: +2 to job (recruitment). The candidate was rejected before Stage II because his father's name was spelled differently on his Class X certificate and his caste certificate. The court ordered the Coast Guard to verify his identity and date of birth. If both were confirmed, he was to be reinstated in the next induction batch, subject to medical clearance. Some coverage describes this as an "Aadhaar discrepancy". Preventable: **yes, at the self-check level** (a cross-document spelling check). Features: **Document Check**. — [SCC Online blog](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2024/11/16/delhi-high-court-orders-indian-coast-guard-verify-candidate-documents-reinstates-induction-legal-news/). *Case name not captured. Pull the order before citing it.*
- **Madhya Pradesh High Court (service matter, date of birth).** The court held that Aadhaar and voter ID are prepared from self-declaration, serve identification only, and are not primary evidence of age. Service records were preferred. Relevance: students should not assume Aadhaar settles a date-of-birth dispute. The Class 10 certificate is usually the anchor document. Features: **Document Check** (explain which document is usually treated as primary, without claiming any authority's rule). — [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/personal-finance/date-of-birth-dispute-mp-high-court-says-aadhaar-voter-id-not-final-proof-service-records-prevail). *Case name and date not captured.*
- **Kerala High Court (student Aadhaar DOB error).** An operator's data-entry error put the wrong date on a student's Aadhaar. Aadhaar centres then rejected both the foreign birth certificate and the CBSE Class X certificate as correction proof. Outcome not captured. — [Kerala Law Academy judgment page](https://cdj.library.keralalawacademy.in/view_judgment1.php?id=1024723).
- **Uttarakhand High Court (2026).** A candidate sought a one-time Aadhaar DOB correction to match her high-school certificate after an earlier update based on a hospital birth certificate. The document title says the correction was allowed under UIDAI SOP clause 4. — [judgment PDF](https://fhwzizvulsqxfzkaqeia.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/judgments/uidai-sop-clause-4-one-time-aadhaar-date-of-birth-correction-allowed-despite-prior-fraudulent-update-uttarakhand-hc-a27f8f.pdf). *Unusual hosting (a Supabase bucket). Verify against the official High Court site.*

### Inferences
- The recurring failure is a mismatch between several documents (Class 10 certificate, Aadhaar, caste/EWS certificate, parent names) that nobody compares until a verification desk does. A **Document Check** that asks the student to type each field exactly as printed on each document, then flags differences, matches the failure pattern without claiming to verify anything.
- Corrections take time (board procedures, Aadhaar update limits). The check is most useful at Class 10 and +2, long before counselling or recruitment.

### Gaps
- No Supreme Court case was found that is specifically about an Aadhaar-versus-Class-10 DOB mismatch cancelling an admission. Search Indian Kanoon directly for this.
- The case names for the Delhi Coast Guard and MP High Court items were not captured.

## 2. Missing, expired or late certificates at counselling or recruitment (caste, EWS, OBC-NCL, income, domicile)

### Takeaway
Certificate validity windows are the sharpest trap. Courts split: relief is likely when the issuing authority caused the delay, and less likely when the student was simply late or held the wrong-period certificate. The 2016 Supreme Court precedent favouring late OBC certificates was referred to a larger bench in 2020.

### Cited Findings
- **Ram Kumar Gijroya v. Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board, Supreme Court, 2016.** Stage: job (Staff Nurse recruitment, Govt. of Delhi). Held that OBC claims can be considered even if the certificate was produced after the advertised cut-off date. Preventable: **partly**. Features: **Document Check** + **My Plan**. — [LiveLaw](https://livelaw.in/amp/top-stories/cut-off-date-caste-certificates-reservation-claims-152068); [SupremeCourtCases](https://www.supremecourtcases.com/ram-kumar-gijroya-v-delhi-subordinate-services-selection-board-anr/amp/)
- **Karn Singh Yadav (Supreme Court, 2020).** A bench of Justices Shantanagoudar and Subhash Reddy referred Gijroya to a larger bench, citing the "administrative chaos" of allowing late certificates. No larger-bench decision was found. — [LiveLaw](https://livelaw.in/amp/top-stories/cut-off-date-caste-certificates-reservation-claims-152068)
- **Delhi High Court, 2025 (AIIMS Security-cum-Fire Guard recruitment).** Relief was refused to candidates whose OBC-NCL certificates were issued outside the advertised window (1 April 2022 – 31 March 2023). The court held the window mandatory and distinguished Gijroya. Stage: job. Preventable: **yes, at the self-check level** (an "is your certificate dated inside the window the notice specifies?" prompt). Features: **Document Check**, **My Plan**. — [Delhi HC judgment PDF](https://delhihighcourt.nic.in/app/showFileJudgment/75510102025CW134282025_191114.pdf); [RawLaw summary](https://rawlaw.in/delhi-high-court-obc-certificates-rejected/)
- **Calcutta High Court, Ujjwal Biswas v. State of West Bengal (WBJEE counselling, 2026).** Stage: +2 → Bachelor's (engineering). The candidate's EWS certificate had expired. The court allowed interim participation in counselling as EWS on condition that he produce a 2026–27 EWS certificate. If the State refused to issue one, he would be treated as General. The university relied on *Mohit Kumar v. State of UP* (Supreme Court) for the rule that a valid reservation certificate is needed on the date of document scrutiny. Preventable: **yes, at the reminder level**. Features: **Document Check** (validity year), **My Plan** (renewal reminder before counselling). — [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/high-court/calcutta-high-court/calcutta-high-court-allows-candidate-to-participate-in-wbjee-counselling-despite-expired-ews-certificate-subject-to-renewal-542763)
- **Calcutta High Court, Priyangshu Mukherjee (NEET-UG 2026).** The court ordered the district magistrate to decide an aspirant's EWS certificate application urgently because document verification was the next day. Stage: +2/Dropper → MBBS. Features: **My Plan** (apply for the certificate weeks ahead). — [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/high-court/calcutta-high-court/calcutta-high-court-directs-urgent-decision-on-neet-aspirants-ews-certificate-says-mothers-voter-list-deletion-not-relevant-546076)
- **Calcutta High Court (NEET-UG 2026).** The court rejected a plea to switch category to EWS after the merit list, because the petitioners held no EWS certificate when they submitted the NEET form. Stage: +2/Dropper. Preventable: **yes, at the self-check level** (category must be decided and supported by documents at application time). Features: **Exam Stack** + **Document Check**. — [CourtBook](https://courtbook.in/posts/neet-ug-2026-calcutta-hc-rejects-plea-to-change-category-to-ews-after-merit-list); [LawArticle](https://lawarticle.in/neet-ug-ews-reservation-category-change/)
- **J&K High Court (MBBS, EWS).** The court ordered an MBBS seat reserved or admission granted where the candidate's EWS certificate was not renewed in time by the authorities. It said a meritorious candidate should not lose a seat over the authority's inaction. — [Greater Kashmir](https://m.greaterkashmir.com/article/hc-orders-reserving-mbbs-seat-for-ews-category-candidate/401965). *Date and case name not captured.*

### Inferences
- EWS and OBC-NCL certificates are typically tied to a financial year. That is a dated, recurring renewal, which suits **My Plan** reminders keyed to the student's exam and counselling calendar.
- The app should present the "certificate validity window" as something to confirm in the official notice. It cannot know each counselling authority's rule.

### Gaps
- Migration certificate and gap-affidavit disputes: no court case was found in this pass.
- Domicile certificate document-timing disputes were not separately sourced. See section 3 for the domicile-quota merits.
- The *Mohit Kumar v. State of UP* citation was seen only as quoted inside the Calcutta report. It was not independently verified.

## 3. Eligibility disputes: subjects, qualifications, equivalence, domicile, attempts

### Takeaway
Courts generally defer to the rule-making body on qualification equivalence (diploma/ITI versus Class 12, subject requirements). A student who assumes equivalence can be shut out. Domicile rules can change by court order, as with PG medical in 2025.

### Cited Findings
- **Devender Bhaskar v. State of Haryana, Supreme Court, 24 November 2021.** Stage: Diploma → job. The High Court had let holders of a Kurukshetra University diploma be considered for Arts and Crafts teacher posts. The Supreme Court set that aside: equivalence is for the recruiting State to decide, and a distance diploma cannot be equated with a regular one. Preventable: **partly** (prompt the student to check whether the specific notice accepts their qualification). Features: **Eligibility** (show "equivalence decided by the notifying authority, confirm in the notice"). — [SCI judgment PDF](https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2007/13481/13481_2007_7_1501_31460_Judgement_24-Nov-2021.pdf); [SupremeCourtCases](https://www.supremecourtcases.com/devender-bhaskar-and-others-v-state-of-haryana-and-others/amp/)
- **Supreme Court, "Court cannot declare equivalency of a course" (Unnikrishnan CV v. Union of India, as reported).** Courts will not substitute their view for expert bodies unless the rule itself prescribes equivalence. No material showed a DED diploma was equivalent to a Civil Engineering diploma. Stage: Diploma → job. — [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/top-stories/supreme-court-judgment-prescribed-educational-qualification-equivalency-of-course-225161). *Date not captured. The case name comes from a search summary; confirm it from the LiveLaw page.*
- **Madras High Court, 2022 (LLB admission).** The Bar Council of India told the court that a 3-year diploma/polytechnic will be treated on par with a Class 12 certificate for LLB admission. Stage: Diploma → Bachelor's. This shows equivalence varies by regulator. — [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/news-updates/madras-high-court-3-year-diploma-course-will-be-treated-on-par-with-12th-certificate-for-admission-to-llb-course-210535)
- **NEET subject eligibility (Biology).** In 2022, NMC told the Supreme Court that NEET candidates must have completed 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Biology and English. — [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/top-stories/neet-candidates-studied-class-11-12-physics-chemistry-biology-english-eligible-nmc-supreme-court-191594). The 2023 position allows Biology/Biotechnology passed as an additional subject at 10+2 from a recognised board. — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/education/news/students-clearing-10-2-without-biology-still-eligible-to-appear-for-neet-123112300195_1.html); [Vikaspedia](https://vikaspedia.in/news/biology-as-additional-subject-for-neet-ug-eligibility). The NMC document references *Anshul Aggrawal v. Union of India* (Delhi HC, 2018), which quashed the bar on open-school/private candidates. — [NMC document via Medical Dialogues](https://medicaldialogues.in/pdf_upload/nmc-neet-eligibility-226022.pdf). Stage: Class 10 (stream choice) → +2 → Dropper. Preventable: **yes, at the self-check level**. Features: **Eligibility** (subject-combination check at stream choice), **Exam Stack**. *A reported High Court ruling (petitioner "Gopal Singh") that Class 11 Biology is no longer mandatory was found only on coaching sites ([Allen](https://news.allen.in/mbbs-admission-class-11-biology-not-mandatory/)). Unverified, do not cite as law.*
- **Tanvi Behl v. Shrey Goel, Supreme Court, 29 January 2025, 2025 INSC 125 (Civil Appeal 9289 of 2019).** Residence/domicile-based reservation in PG medical courses held unconstitutional under Article 14. The case arose from GMCH Chandigarh's 64-seat State Quota. Reports say students already admitted under such reservation are unaffected. Stage: Bachelor's → Master's (PG medical). Preventable: **no** (a rule change). Relevant to **Eligibility**: domicile rules can change, so show the date each rule was last checked. — [SCI judgment PDF](https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2019/15961/15961_2019_4_1502_59041_Judgement_29-Jan-2025.pdf); [Verdictum](https://www.verdictum.in/app-lite/court-updates/supreme-court/domicile-based-reservation-is-constitutionally-impermissible-in-pg-medical-courses-tanvi-behl-v-shrey-goel-2025-insc-125-1566383?hasShare=1); [SC Observer](https://www.scobserver.in/?p=15687)

### Inferences
- The highest-leverage point is **Class 10 stream choice**. Subject combinations (Maths, Biology) decide later exam eligibility. An **Eligibility** check that shows "this stream keeps / closes these exams" is well supported by these cases.
- For Diploma/ITI students, the app should never state that a qualification is equivalent to Class 12 in general. It should say equivalence is regulator- and notice-specific.

### Gaps
- No primary source was found in this pass for: the JEE 75% board criterion litigation, NEET/JEE attempt-limit litigation, or ITI-equivalence cases. These need targeted Indian Kanoon searches.

## 4. Counselling and seat errors, fee deadlines, refunds, unapproved/fake institutions

### Takeaway
Several Supreme Court cases involve students who lost IIT seats over portal clicks or minutes-late fee payments. Relief came only through Article 142 and was expressly not precedent. High Courts often refuse relief for late payment. UGC publishes a fee refund policy and a fake-university list, but both must be checked by the student.

### Cited Findings
- **Siddhant Batra v. IIT Bombay, Supreme Court, 9 December 2020.** Stage: +2 → Bachelor's (JoSAA). A student with JEE Advanced rank 270 meant to freeze his electrical engineering seat at IIT Bombay but clicked a link to withdraw from JoSAA seat allocation (31 October 2020). The Bombay HC dismissed his petition (23 November 2020). The Supreme Court (Kaul, Maheshwari, Roy JJ.) ordered interim admission, and later reports say the admission was regularised. Preventable: **partly** (a "what each JoSAA button means: freeze / float / slide / withdraw" explainer and a warning before irreversible choices). Features: **New feature needed: Counselling step explainer** (plain-language guide to each counselling option and its consequences; it cannot act on the portal). — [LiveLaw](https://livelaw.in/top-stories/iit-bombay-supreme-court-student-admission-wrong-click-167019); [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/student-loses-iit-bombay-seat-due-wrong-click-moves-sc); [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Fsc-asks-iit-bombay-to-grant-regular-admission-to-student-who-mistakenly-withdrew-seat-935830.html)
- **Prince Jaibir Singh (IIT Bombay), Supreme Court (Chandrachud and Bopanna JJ.).** Stage: +2 → Bachelor's. The student was allotted civil engineering at IIT Bombay on 27 October and uploaded documents on 29 October, but could not pay the fee in time. Reports cite a shortage of money and technical errors. The Bombay HC dismissed his petition. The Supreme Court used Article 142 to order a supernumerary seat, saying the order was not precedent. Year: reports are from 2021. Preventable: **partly**. Features: **My Plan** (fee-deadline reminder), **Cost Loan & ROI Calculator** (know the acceptance fee in advance), **Parent Mode**. — [Scroll](https://scroll.in/latest/1011191/sc-asks-iit-bombay-to-create-seat-for-dalit-student-who-could-not-pay-his-fee-on-time); [The Leaflet](https://theleaflet.in/supreme-court/sc-asks-iit-bombay-to-adopt-humanitarian-approach-and-allot-seat-to-dalit-boy-in-48-hours)
- **Atul Kumar v. IIT Dhanbad / Joint Seat Allocation Authority, Supreme Court, 30 September 2024** (Chandrachud CJI, Pardiwala and Misra JJ.). Stage: +2/Dropper → Bachelor's. A JEE Advanced 2024 rank-1455 student from a BPL family could not pay the ₹17,500 acceptance fee before the 5 PM deadline on 24 June 2024. The money was arranged at 4:45 PM. The Supreme Court used Article 142 to order admission to B.Tech Electrical through a supernumerary seat. Preventable: **partly** (early awareness of the exact fee and deadline; the root cause was poverty). Features: **My Plan**, **Cost Loan & ROI Calculator**, **Parent Mode**, plus linking to fee waivers where officially stated. — [Bar & Bench](https://barandbench.com/news/supreme-court-comes-aid-dalit-student-couldnt-pay-iit-admission-fee); [SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2024/10/01/supreme-court-invokes-article-142-dalit-student-iit-fee-issue-grants-admission-original-allotment-batch/); [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/india/sc-orders-iit-dhanbad-to-admit-dalit-student-who-could-not-deposit-fees-in-time)
- **Karnataka High Court, NEET-UG 2026 (April 2026).** The court refused to reopen the payment portal for a candidate who waited until the last date and claimed a portal glitch. It distinguished the Supreme Court's relief as resting on "peculiar facts" and noted the petitioner waited about a month before going to court. — [Lawyer E News](https://lawyerenews.com/legal_detail/neet-ug-2026-karnataka-high-court-refuses-to-reopen-payment-portal-for-candidate-who-waited-till-last-date-to-pay-fees)
- **Bombay High Court.** Relief was denied to a meritorious student who submitted an admission form late: relief "cannot be granted merely on the ground of sympathy". — [LawChakra](https://lawchakra.in/high-court/hc-denies-relief-student-missing-admission-deadline/). *Date not captured.*
- **UGC Fee Refund Policy 2024-25 (letter dated 12 June 2024).** Full refund for cancellations or migrations up to 30 September 2024. Up to ₹1,000 processing fee for cancellations up to 31 October 2024. After that, the October 2018 UGC notification's tiered scale applies: 100% if 15+ days before the last admission date, 90% if under 15 days before, 80% if 15 days or less after, 50% if 15–30 days after, and 0% beyond 30 days. Stated to continue until revised. Stage: Bachelor's / Master's. Features: **Cost Loan & ROI Calculator** (refund-window display), **My Plan** (refund deadline). — [All India Radio News](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/ugc-announces-new-fee-refund-policy-for-higher-education-institutions); [NFSU copy of UGC policy PDF](https://beta.nfsu.ac.in/data/pdfs/admission/Fee-Refund-Policy-2024-25_Announced%20by%20UGC.pdf); [Tribune, 2018 notification](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/india/refund-fee-to-students-who-cancel-admission-ugc-to-varsities-colleges-630941/amp). *Whether a newer 2025-26 or 2026-27 policy exists was not confirmed. Check ugc.gov.in.*
- **UGC fake university notices.** A January 2025 list named 21 fake universities (Delhi 8, Uttar Pradesh 4). A December 2025 notice warned against three "self-styled" institutions in Delhi, Maharashtra and Karnataka (including National Institute of Management Solution, Delhi) as not entitled to award degrees. The official list is at ugc.gov.in/universitydetails/Fakeuniversity, which Parliament material also references. Stage: +2 → Bachelor's. Preventable: **yes, at the self-check level**. Features: **Explore** (link to the official list with the date last checked; never claim real-time status). — [Careers360, Dec 2025](https://news.careers360.com/ugc-warns-students-about-3-self-styled-fake-institutions-delhi-maharashtra-karnataka-degree-not-valid-aicte-act); [ETV Bharat, Jan 2025](https://etvbharat.com/en/!state/beware-of-these-21-fake-universities-in-india-enn25010702964); [Sansad document](https://eparlib.sansad.in/bitstream/123456789/2989033/1/AU195_kffQdQ.pdf); [Patrika, Aug 2026](https://www.patrika.com/en/education-news/ugc-exposes-21-fake-universities-across-india-delhi-and-kolkata-top-the-list-19296283). *Counts differ by date (21, 22, 24 in an older 2018 list). Use the UGC page as the authority.*
- **Unapproved or disaffiliated colleges.** The AP High Court (2026) upheld a law college's disaffiliation over infrastructure deficiencies but protected existing students by making it effective from 2026-27. — [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/high-court/andhra-pradesh-high-court/ap-high-court-upholds-disaffiliation-law-college-violating-bci-ugc-norms-543675). The Supreme Court (2017) told High Courts not to allow medical and dental admissions pending affiliation disputes. — [Hindustan Times via PressReader](https://www.pressreader.com/india/hindustan-times-chandigarh/20170412/281500751115312). The Hyderabad HC (2015) told a technical university to accommodate students who had chosen colleges denied affiliation (older). — [Deccan Chronicle](https://deccanchronicle.com/amp/150904/nation-current-affairs/article/hyderabad-high-court-breather-tech-students). Pondicherry University warned students against an unaffiliated B.Ed college. — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/pondicherry-university-warns-students-against-unaffiliated-usha-latchumanan-college-of-education). Features: **Explore** (prompt the student to check the AICTE/NCTE/BCI/NMC approval for the current year).

### Inferences
- Supreme Court relief in the IIT cases was **discretionary (Article 142) and expressly non-precedential**, while High Courts often refuse late-payment relief. The app's message should be "don't rely on courts". A deadline reminder plus a known fee amount is the realistic prevention.
- The counselling-click failure (Batra) is not covered by any current feature. It needs a counselling-step explainer. The app must not automate portal actions or claim to know the portal's current interface.

### Gaps
- No consumer-forum or court case on UGC refund non-compliance was captured in this pass.
- No case was found of a student's degree being invalidated because of an AICTE-unapproved programme in 2015–2026 with full details. A Careers360 Q&A references an older deemed-university matter (JRN Rajasthan Vidyapeeth, 2007 B.Tech), which is secondhand and old.

## 5. Scholarship denials due to documents or deadlines (NSP, state portals) and audits

### Takeaway
No High Court judgment specifically about NSP was found. The closest case is a 2025 Delhi HC INSPIRE scholarship order where the department's late notification caused the miss. CAG audits document systemic failures that hurt eligible students as well: delays of one to six years, short reimbursement and denial.

### Cited Findings
- **Delhi High Court, May 2025, INSPIRE scholarship.** Stage: Bachelor's. A student received a provisional offer but missed the document-upload window. The notification email arrived after the link had expired, and the advertisement required email notice. The court directed the government to grant three years of INSPIRE scholarship. Preventable: **partly** (a reminder to check the portal directly and not wait for email). Features: **My Plan**. — [SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2025/05/29/dhc-directs-govt-to-grant-three-years-inspire-scholarship-to-a-girl-student/)
- **CAG Performance Audit Report No. 12 of 2018, Post-Matric Scholarships for SC students.** Covered Karnataka, Maharashtra, Punjab, Tamil Nadu and UP for 2012–17 (period pre-2015, flagged). It found irregularities in disbursement involving ₹581.68 crore, portal data discrepancies of ₹455.98 crore, denial or short reimbursement, payments to ineligible students, and **delays of one to six years in paying 18.58 lakh students in four states**. Stage: +2 to Master's. Preventable by app: **no** for systemic delay. **Partly** for individual document readiness. — [CAG press release](https://cag.gov.in/uploads/PressRelease/PR-Press-12-of-2018-05f5f6f2d446443-27846945.pdf); [CAG conclusions](https://cag.gov.in/uploads/download_audit_report/2018/Part_3_Conclusion_and_Recommendations_of_Report_No_12_of_2018_-_Performance_Audit_on_Post_Matric_Scholarships_to_the_Students_belonging_to_Scheduled_Castes_for_st.pdf)
- **CAG (reported December 2025), minority pre- and post-matric scholarships.** At least ₹9 crore went to more than 11,000 untraceable beneficiaries or students at non-operational schools. Awards were paid despite unmet income or marks criteria. Funds spilled over across financial years. The report cites "sole reliance" on NSP for monitoring. — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/pre-post-matric-scholarship-minority-ineligible-fake-beneficiaries-thousands-nsp-national-scholarship-portal-crore-cag-report)
- **CAG Report No. 3 of 2020 (state compliance audit).** Includes a post-matric SC/OBC scholarship review: inadequate scrutiny, irregular disbursement, suspected fraudulent payment, weak monitoring. — [CAG report PDF](https://cag.gov.in/uploads/download_audit_report/2019/Report%20No.%203%20of%202020_N-PSUs_English_2018-19-06050acc3c76178.26470394.pdf). *The state was not captured. Confirm it from the report.*
- **Related deadline cases (not scholarship).** The Delhi HC allowed 16 students to sit CBSE compartment exams despite a missed upload caused by a teacher's medical emergency. — [SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/?p=326631). ### Inferences
- Courts tend to help when the authority failed to communicate and the student acted promptly. They tend to refuse when the student was simply late. **My Plan** reminders and a "save proof of submission" prompt support the student's position either way.

## 6. Official reports on exam conduct (NTA, 2024–2026)

### Takeaway
Exam-system failures (NEET-UG 2024 grace marks and leak allegations, NEET-UG 2026 cancellation) are not preventable by students. An app can still reduce harm by tracking re-exam dates and official notices.

### Cited Findings
- **NEET-UG 2024 grace marks.** On 13 June 2024 the Centre/NTA told the Supreme Court (Vikram Nath and Sandeep Mehta JJ.) that grace marks given to 1,563 candidates would be cancelled, with an optional re-test. Those who did not take it would keep their 5 May scores without grace marks. The court did not stay counselling. Petitions included Vanshika Yadav v. UoI and Shivangi Mishra v. NTA. Stage: +2/Dropper. Preventable: **no**. Features: **Exam Stack** (official-notice tracking, re-test date). — [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/top-stories/neet-ug-2024-grace-marks-given-to-1563-candidates-will-be-cancelled-theyll-be-given-retest-option-centre-tells-supreme-court-260388); [SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2024/06/13/sc-neet-ug-2024-grace-marks-cancelled-option-retest-scc-times/); [All India Radio News](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/grace-marks-awarded-to-over-1500-students-in-neet-ug-2024-examination-to-be-cancelled-centre-informs-supreme-court). *The final disposition of these petitions was not captured in this pass.*

Mandate: reform of the examination process, data security, and NTA structure. ### Inferences
- For exam-conduct failures, the honest app role is "official notice tracker plus backup plan". This fits Mārgadarshak's "credible backup" principle: if NEET is disrupted, show the student's other open routes.

### New feature needed (from this evidence)
- **Counselling step explainer:** a plain-language guide to JoSAA, MCC and state counselling options (freeze, float, slide, withdraw, surrender) and the deadline and fee attached to each step, with a "this action may be irreversible, read the official instructions" warning. It must not automate the portal or claim to know the current portal interface.
- **Certificate validity tracker** (could live inside Document Check + My Plan): records the issue date of each certificate the student holds (EWS, OBC-NCL, income, domicile) and reminds the student to confirm the validity window in each notice. It must not say a certificate "is valid".
