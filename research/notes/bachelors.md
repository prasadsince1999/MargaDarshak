# Indian Bachelor's students and recent graduates: problems by stage

Research date: 20 October 2026. Scope: India only. Web search + selective page fetches (about 16 tool calls). Several figures come via secondary aggregators (news/coaching summaries); this is flagged inline. No quotes or statistics were invented; anything without a source sits in Gaps.

Feature vocabulary used for tagging: Compare Paths, What If, Eligibility, Exam Stack, Cost Loan & ROI Calculator, Document Check, Foundation Check, Explore, My Plan, Parent Mode, or "new feature needed".

## During the degree (Year 1 to final year): what goes wrong?

### Takeaway
The strongest documented in-degree problems are: placement access that depends on college tier and on rigid eligibility filters (no active backlogs, 60-75% cut-offs); NEP four-year UG rules that changed mid-session and confuse exit/equivalence decisions; recognition risk (UGC fake-university list); and scholarship delays that lead colleges to withhold marksheets and degrees. "Which skills matter in which year" is a widely asked question, but I found no authoritative year-by-year dataset.

### Cited Findings

**P1. Placement access depends on the college tier; tier-2/3 campus pipelines rely on mass IT-services recruiters**
- Stage: Bachelor's Year 3 to final year (mainly BTech/BCA/BSc CS).
- Evidence: The Quint reports that placement offices at tier-2/3 colleges were built around mass drives by TCS, Infosys, Wipro and Cognizant, and that these firms scaled back. It cites an Economic Times figure of a 50-70% fall in placements at private engineering colleges (secondhand; not verified at primary source). — [The Quint](https://www.thequint.com/amp/story/jobs/campus-placement-jobs-fall-economic-crisis-unemployment-iit-iim-ai-degrees)
- Counter-evidence: HirePro, via Business Standard, describes a strong FY26 placement season after a two-year lull. Wipro planned 10,000-12,000 campus hires in FY26. — [HirePro / Business Standard](https://hirepro.in/engineering-colleges-witness-strong-placement-season-after-two-year-lull); [Business Standard campus-hiring topic](https://www.business-standard.com/topic/campus-hiring). **The sources conflict; the trend for 2025-26 is unsettled.**
- Real student phrasing (Careers360 question titles): a BTech Biotechnology student at a third-tier college, "no placements absolute 0"; "should I leave the course? please answer honestly". — [Careers360](https://www.careers360.com/question-i-am-rohit-studying-btech-biotechnology-at-a-3rd-tier-college-there-is-no-placements-absolute-0-my-financial-condition-is-very-poori-came-to-this-college-thinking-of-there-would-be-some-placements-but-unfortunately-notno-return-of-investment-should-i-leave-the-course-please-answer-honestly/amp)
- App fit: **Partly.** Features: **Explore** (off-campus routes such as apprenticeships, GATE, PSU, MBA), **My Plan** (a skill/project checklist by semester), and **Cost Loan & ROI Calculator** (for the "no return of investment" fear). The app cannot create placements.

**P2. Backlogs and percentage cut-offs silently exclude students from campus drives**
- Stage: Bachelor's Year 1 to final year. The damage happens at the first backlog; it shows up in the final year.
- Evidence: One college's 2023-24 BTech placement table shows ICICI Bank requiring 60% with no live backlogs, and L&T requiring 65% with no backlogs at any point (cleared on the first attempt). — [SPCE placement statistics](https://www.spce.ac.in/placement_stat.php)
- Cognizant GenC allows no active backlog; Accenture requires no active backlog at application or interview (blog summary; 2023-24 criteria; verify current). — [PlacementPreparation.io](https://www.placementpreparation.io/blog-live/?p=6540)
- App fit: **Yes.** Feature: **Eligibility**, degree version. The student enters CGPA/percentage, active and cleared backlogs and the year-gap history. The app shows which recruiter or exam filters they currently pass or fail, labelled as typical stated criteria rather than a guarantee. Add a **What If** scenario such as "if I clear 2 backlogs by semester 7". **New sub-feature needed: a "Backlog impact" warning in My Plan.**

**P3. NEP 2020 four-year UG and multiple-entry/exit (ME-ME) confusion**
- Stage: Bachelor's Year 1 (choosing the track), Year 3 end (exit or continue), and Year 4.
- Evidence: Delhi University's ME-ME scheme sets exit points of a certificate (44 credits), a diploma (88), a 3-year degree (132) and a 4-year degree (176), with re-entry allowed within seven years. — [The Week / PTI, July 2025](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/national/2025/07/31/des109-dl-du-fyup-guidelines.html)
- Over 60% of DU's enrolled students (over 43,000) opted out of the fourth year in 2026. More than 47,600 opted out across FYUP-implementing central universities. The Ministry's reasons were professional admissions such as an MBA, employment after three years, and personal circumstances (Rajya Sabha reply, reported 22 July 2026). — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/over-60pc-delhi-university-du-students-opt-out-4th-year-fyup-ba-bcom-bsc-hnbgu-bbau-lucknow-nep-education-ministry)
- Teachers and students say DU kept changing rules mid-session. — [The Week / PTI](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/national/2025/07/31/des109-dl-du-fyup-guidelines.html)
- Equivalence trap: DU's FAQ says a 4-year BSc (Hons) Computer Science with Research is not equivalent to a BE/BTech, particularly for government recruitment. — [DU Academic Affairs FAQ](https://academicaffairs.du.ac.in/faqs/)
- App fit: **Yes.** Features: **Compare Paths** (3-year exit vs 4th-year Honours vs Honours-with-Research), **What If** ("if I exit after year 3, can I still do a 1-year MA/MTech?"), and **Eligibility** (equivalence flags for government posts). **New feature needed: an "NEP credit and exit tracker"** that records credits earned against exit thresholds, the re-entry deadline and university-specific rules. It must be scoped per university, because rules differ.

**P4. Studying at an unrecognised or fake university**
- Stage: Before admission and Year 1 (often discovered at a job or higher-study verification).
- Evidence: UGC's October 2025 updated notice lists 22 fake universities, 10 of them in Delhi and 4 in Uttar Pradesh. They are not recognised under the UGC Act 1956 and cannot award degrees. — [Organiser, 30 Oct 2025](https://organiser.org/2025/10/30/323385/bharat/ugc-releases-updated-list-of-22-fake-universities-across-india-delhi-tops-the-chart/); a January 2025 list of 21 — [Patrika](https://patrika.com/en/education-news/ugc-exposes-21-fake-universities-across-india-delhi-and-kolkata-top-the-list-19296283?amp=1). UGC advises checking ugc.gov.in. Secondary reporting was used; the primary UGC notice was not fetched.
- App fit: **Yes.** Feature: **Document Check** (a college recognition self-check that links to the official UGC list and the AICTE approval portal). It must state that this is a self-check against an official list, not certification.

**P5. Scholarship delays lead colleges to withhold marksheets and degrees; renewal risk**
- Stage: Every year of the Bachelor's (renewal), and completed graduates (certificates withheld).
- Evidence: In Punjab, the Centre's delay of over Rs 110 crore under the SC Post-Matric Scholarship led private technical colleges to withhold detailed marksheets for 2023-24. The Punjab & Haryana High Court raised concern over degrees withheld from "hundreds of students". — [Tribune India](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/punjab/pvt-colleges-withhold-marksheets-as-centre-delays-sc-scholarship-funds); [Tribune, court](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/punjab/court-seeks-clarity-from-punjab-on-withheld-degrees-hints-at-compensation-for-students/amp)
- In Telangana (Aug 2025), 1.49 lakh minority scholarship applications were pending. Graduates could not collect certificates until fees were cleared. — [The South First](https://thesouthfirst.com/news/1-49-lakh-scholarship-applications-pending-despite-rs-300-crore-allocation-for-telangana-minorities)
- In Telangana in 2022-23, only 65,434 students got fee reimbursement, almost half the 2021-22 number (2023 report). — [The News Minute](https://www.thenewsminute.com/article/students-bind-telangana-govt-delays-post-matric-scholarships-181711)
- The NCSC (Haryana, 2023) directed that institutions must not bar students from exams while scholarship disbursement is pending. — [NCSC press note PDF](https://medicaldialogues.in/pdf_upload/latestpressnotepms-205581.pdf)
- App fit: **Partly.** Features: **My Plan** (renewal deadlines, a marks/attendance threshold reminder), **Document Check** (a renewal document list), and **Eligibility** (renewal conditions, already modelled in Mārgadarshak's scholarship ledger). **New feature needed: a "Scholarship status and rights" card.** It records application/disbursement status and lists official grievance routes (e.g. NCSC direction). The app cannot release funds.

**P6. Not knowing which skills or internships matter by year**
- Stage: Year 1 to Year 3.
- Evidence: Generic guidance only. Forum answers stress self-built skills, projects, referrals and alumni over reliance on the college. — [Careers360 BCA question](https://www.careers360.com/question-guys-i-need-advice-for-my-mca-i-only-know-some-development-skills-html-css-js-and-a-few-basics-of-java-and-python-i-completed-my-bca-from-a-tier-3-college-with-no-placements-right-now-i-am-highly-confused-and-dont-know-what-to-do-i-have-been-continuously-searching-for-different-universities-i-cracke); [JobRise tier-2/3 guide (commercial blog)](https://jobrise.io/blog/tier-2-3-college-placement-tips)
- The VisionIAS summary of an Indian Express column says fewer than 5% of PM Internship Scheme applicants secured internships (year/source not given; weak). — [VisionIAS summary](https://dce.visionias.in/upsc-daily-news-summary/article/2025-10-10/the-indian-express/economy/india-is-staring-at-one-of-its-biggest-crises-unemployment-of-the-educated)
- India Skills Report 2025 (CII/Wheebox/AICTE) puts projected employability at about 54.8%, up from about 51.25%. By discipline: management 78%, engineering 71.5%, MCA 71%, science 58%. Women's projected employability fell to 47.5% from 50.9%. — [Vajiram summary](https://vajiramandravi.com/current-affairs/india-skills-report-2025/); [Morung Express / PTI](https://morungexpress.com/7-pc-rise-in-employability-among-indian-graduates-this-year-report); [Taggd ISR 2025 page](https://taggd.in/industry-reports/isr/india-skills-report-2025/)
- App fit: **Partly.** Feature: **Foundation Check**, degree version (a self-check of core skills by target path, e.g. DSA basics for software, accounting tools for BCom). Pair it with a **My Plan** semester-wise checklist. The year-by-year content must be editorial and labelled, not presented as a verified employer requirement.

**P7. Branch/course regret and the wish to switch or drop out**
- Stage: Year 1 to Year 2.
- Evidence: Only anecdotal question titles were found (P1 Careers360 "should I leave the course?"; a Reddit-mirror post from a low-tier college student weighing GATE/M.Tech vs SSC CGL/UPSC/BPSC). — [r/btech mirror](https://r.datuan.dev/r/btech/comments/1uff96n/help/) (mirror, not the original Reddit; treat cautiously).
- App fit: **Partly.** Features: **Compare Paths** (stay and add a minor/skill vs switch branch vs lateral exit) and **What If** (cost and time of switching).

### Inferences
- The in-degree problems that suit an app best are rule-shaped: backlog and cut-off filters, NEP exit credits, recognition lists and scholarship renewal conditions. These map onto existing Eligibility, Document Check and My Plan logic.
- Placement scarcity and skill gaps are only partly solvable. The honest app role is showing alternatives and costs, not promising jobs.
- The ISR employability numbers come from a test-based projection by an industry body. They are not an outcomes measure, so they should not be shown as job probabilities.

### Gaps
- No primary dataset on branch-switch or dropout rates for Bachelor's students. AISHE dropout or GER figures were not retrieved in this pass.
- No authoritative "skills by year" framework for Indian UG students was found.
- No primary data on how many students lose campus eligibility to backlogs.
- I did not fetch the primary UGC fake-university notice or the AICTE unapproved-institution list.
- Reddit threads (r/developersIndia, r/Indian_Academia, r/IndianStudents) were not directly retrievable via search. Student phrasings rely on Careers360 question titles.

## After the degree: what goes wrong?

### Takeaway
Graduate unemployment is the highest of any education group in India: 29.1% for youth graduates in 2022 (ILO/IHD). Official graduate usual-status rates were 13-15% in 2020-23. Recent graduates face a lopsided choice. Government exams draw hundreds of applicants per post (SSC CGL 2025: about 28 lakh registrations for 14,582 posts). Private jobs bring offer and BGV paperwork risks, and fake-job/training-fee scams target freshers.

### Cited Findings

**A1. Graduate unemployment is structurally high**
- Stage: Completed Bachelor's (0-2 years out).
- Evidence: The IHD-ILO India Employment Report 2024 gives a youth unemployment rate of 29.1% for graduates, versus 3.4% for those who cannot read or write. Youth with secondary education or higher had 18.4%. — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/asia/2024/03/30/young-people-india-more-likely-unemployed-if-educated-international-labour-organization); [Drishti IAS](https://www.drishtiias.com/printpdf/india-employment-report-2024-ilo)
- The share of educated youth among unemployed youth rose from 35.2% in 2000 to 65.7% in 2022. — [GKToday](https://www.gktoday.in/india-employment-report-2024/)
- Young women not in employment, education or training (NEET) were 48.4%, versus 9.8% for young men (2022). — [Drishti IAS](https://www.drishtiias.com/printpdf/india-employment-report-2024-ilo)
- The PLFS usual-status unemployment rate for graduates was 15.5% (2020-21), 14.9% (2021-22) and 13.4% (2022-23), per a government document on the DGE site. — [DGE PDF](https://dge.gov.in/dge/sites/default/files/2024-07/727_e.pdf) (figure reported via search summary; confirm in the PDF).
- An Indian Express column (Oct 2025, via VisionIAS summary) argues that unemployment of the educated is one of India's biggest crises. It cites 46,000 graduates applying for contractual sanitation jobs in Haryana (2024) and fresh-graduate salaries stuck at Rs 3-4 LPA. These are unsourced in the summary; treat them as opinion-grade. — [VisionIAS summary](https://dce.visionias.in/upsc-daily-news-summary/article/2025-10-10/the-indian-express/economy/india-is-staring-at-one-of-its-biggest-crises-unemployment-of-the-educated)
- App fit: **Partly.** Features: **Explore** (realistic first-job routes by degree), **Compare Paths**, and **Cost Loan & ROI Calculator**. Unemployment itself is not app-solvable.

**A2. Job vs government exams vs Master's vs abroad: the post-degree fork**
- Stage: Final year to completed.
- Evidence: A final-year BTech CSE student's options include PSUs via GATE (ONGC, NTPC, BHEL), DRDO/ISRO/BARC, railways, and UPSC or state civil services (Careers360, Jan 2026). — [Careers360](https://www.careers360.com/question-as-a-final-year-student-in-btech-computer-science-and-engineering-what-are-the-government-jobs-that-i-can-apply-for-even-though-i-am-getting-my-degree-in-june-of-this-year/amp)
- Real phrasing (Careers360 title, BTech 3rd-year CSE): "lot of confusion to go with private jobs or govt jobs?" — [Careers360](https://www.careers360.com/question-i-am-studying-btech-3yr-cse-branch-i-have-lot-of-confusion-to-go-with-private-jobs-or-govt-jobs)
- Real phrasing (BCA graduate, tier 3): "highly confused and dont know what to do". — [Careers360](https://www.careers360.com/question-guys-i-need-advice-for-my-mca-i-only-know-some-development-skills-html-css-js-and-a-few-basics-of-java-and-python-i-completed-my-bca-from-a-tier-3-college-with-no-placements-right-now-i-am-highly-confused-and-dont-know-what-to-do-i-have-been-continuously-searching-for-different-universities-i-cracke)
- Fourth-year opt-outs show students leaving for MBA or jobs after three years (see P3). — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/over-60pc-delhi-university-du-students-opt-out-4th-year-fyup-ba-bcom-bsc-hnbgu-bbau-lucknow-nep-education-ministry)
- App fit: **Yes.** This is the core Mārgadarshak use case. Features: **Compare Paths**, degree version (Job now / Government exam / Master's via GATE, CAT, CUET-PG or JAM / Abroad, plus a backup). Add **Exam Stack** (which exams a BSc or BCom graduate can stack in one year without conflict) and **Eligibility** (age, attempts, degree and percent). Add **Cost Loan & ROI Calculator** for MBA/MTech/abroad.

**A3. Years lost to government exam preparation**
- Stage: Completed (often 1-5+ years after the degree).
- Evidence: SSC CGL 2025 had about 28.15 lakh registrations for 14,582 vacancies, roughly 193 per post. 2024 had 34.83 lakh registrations and 2023 had 24.74 lakh. — [Adda247](https://www.adda247.com/exams/ssc/ssc-received-28-lakh-applications-for-cgl-posts-against-14582-vacancies/) (coaching site; no official SSC confirmation found); vacancy count — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/ssc-cgl-2025-14582-vacancies-for-group-b-c-combined-graduate-level-posts-tier-one-exam-from-august-13-to-30/amp)
- UPSC CSE draws about 10-13 lakh applicants a year for 800-1,000 vacancies. A 2017 CSDS survey found over 65% of youth prefer government jobs (older; flag). — [Siasat](https://www.siasat.com/between-hope-and-hardship-why-are-millions-of-youth-chasing-government-jobs-3250123/)
- Aspirants keep trying "till their eligibility is exhausted" (journalism; 2016-17 data, older). — [The Quint](https://www.thequint.com/news/india/upsc-results-students-failure)
- App fit: **Yes, for the decision; no, for the outcome.** Features: **What If** ("if I take 2 attempts and fail, where am I at age 26?"), **Exam Stack** (pairing a primary exam with backups that share a syllabus), and **Eligibility** (remaining attempts and age limit). **New feature needed: an "Attempt budget and exit point".** The student sets a maximum number of years or attempts upfront, with a pre-committed backup path and skill-retention checkpoints in My Plan. **Parent Mode** fits here because family pressure drives these decisions, but stay non-presumptive per the psychology contract.

**A4. Fake job offers and training-fee scams aimed at freshers**
- Stage: Final year to completed.
- Evidence: The pattern is fees labelled "registration", "training" or "security deposit", with fake offer letters that use company logos. Freshers are targeted for their lack of exposure. — [BOOM Live](https://www.boomlive.in/decode/scamcheck/job-offer-or-job-fraud-heres-how-to-tell-29305); [CielHR](https://www.cielhr.com/spotting-the-scam-fake-hiring-practices-in-indias-job-market)
- Some victims are lured abroad with fake offers into forced cybercrime operations (secondary). — [JobRise 2026](https://jobrise.io/en/blog/job-scams-india-avoid-2026/)
- Statistics on scale (a claimed 3x rise; Rs 150 crore lost) are unverified single-source commercial claims and are excluded.
- App fit: **Partly.** **New feature needed: a "Job offer red-flag check".** This is a self-check list (asks for money? no interview? personal email domain?) with links to cybercrime.gov.in and helpline 1930. Confirm the official channel before shipping. It cannot verify an employer.

**A5. Degree verification, name/marks mismatch and withheld certificates at onboarding**
- Stage: Completed (offer to joining).
- Evidence: Spelling differences across PAN, Aadhaar and degree certificates commonly delay background verification (BGV); most "failures" are documentation mismatches. — [Instahyre](https://resources.instahyre.com/blog/background-verification-process-india/)
- A resume showing 75% when the official aggregate is 73.8% gets flagged. — [JobRise BGV guide](https://jobrise.io/en/blog/background-verification-india-guide/)
- AuthBridge reported a 48% rise in employee-credential discrepancies (year not confirmed; likely older). — [People Matters](https://www.peoplematters.in/article/background-verification/discrepancy-in-employee-credentials-rise-48-authbridge-report-16884)
- Withheld degrees over unpaid scholarship fees (see P5) block onboarding.
- App fit: **Yes.** Feature: **Document Check**, graduate version. It compares name, date of birth and father's name across Class 10 certificate, degree, Aadhaar and PAN; checks provisional vs final degree, consolidated marksheet, migration certificate and percentage-from-CGPA conversion; and gives correction steps (affidavit or gazette). Fully local and self-reported.

**A6. Placement offers that are delayed or revoked, and fresher layoffs**
- Stage: Final year to completed.
- Evidence: Business Standard reported 350 Infosys freshers let go after failing internal tests. — [Business Standard campus-hiring topic](https://www.business-standard.com/topic/campus-hiring) (via search summary; date not confirmed).
- App fit: **Partly.** Feature: **My Plan** (a backup path active while waiting for a joining date).

### Feature fit: what does NOT fit degree students, and the degree-specific replacement
| School-stage feature/content | Why it does not fit a Bachelor's student | Degree-specific version |
|---|---|---|
| Compare Paths: stream comparison (PCM vs PCB vs Commerce vs Arts) | Stream is already locked | Compare Paths: Job vs Govt exam vs Master's (GATE/CAT/CUET-PG/JAM) vs Abroad vs NEP 3-yr exit vs 4th year |
| Exam Stack: JEE/NEET/CUET-UG stacking | Entrance exams already over | Exam Stack: GATE + PSU, CAT + bank PO, SSC CGL + state PSC, CUET-PG + JAM (shared-syllabus clusters, date conflicts) |
| Eligibility: Class 12 subject/percent rules | The binding filters are now CGPA, backlogs, age and attempts | Eligibility: CGPA-to-% conversion, active/cleared backlogs, age/attempt limits, NEP degree equivalence |
| Foundation Check: school subject basics | Wrong level | Foundation Check: employability self-check by target path (labelled editorial; ISR-style skills, not a certified test) |
| Document Check: Class 10/12 certificates, caste/income for school schemes | Incomplete | Document Check: recognition of college (UGC/AICTE), provisional/final degree, migration, name consistency across documents for BGV |
| Parent Mode: school-choice framing | Graduate is an adult; the parent role shifts | Parent Mode: shared view of the attempt budget and backup plan, without exposing the student's private data by default |
| Cost Loan & ROI: school coaching costs | Different decision | ROI for MBA/MTech/MSc abroad and for government-exam coaching years (opportunity cost of foregone salary) |

New features needed, summarised: (1) NEP credit and exit tracker; (2) Backlog impact warning in My Plan; (3) Scholarship status and rights card; (4) Attempt budget and exit point for government exams; (5) Job offer red-flag check; (6) Graduate Document Check (BGV readiness).

### Inferences
- The highest-value degree-stage features are Compare Paths (post-degree fork) plus Exam Stack and an attempt budget, because the documented harm (unemployment, years in exam prep) sits at that fork.
- Document and recognition problems are cheap to address with local self-checks and match Mārgadarshak's existing Document Check pattern. Copy must say "self-check", not "verified".
- Any unemployment or employability figure shown in-app needs its year and survey definition (PLFS usual status vs ILO youth rate). The two differ widely (13-15% vs 29.1%) because they measure different populations.

### Gaps
- PLFS 2024 and 2025 graduate-specific unemployment rates were not found. The latest found is 2022-23 (13.4%). One summary claiming 6.5% for "secondary and above" in 2025 conflicts with the 3.1% overall figure and was not verified.
- No rigorous study quantifies the years lost to government-exam preparation. Only journalism and opinion pieces were found.
- No primary data on the scale of fresher job scams (NCRB or I4C figures were not retrieved).
- No data on how often name mismatches lead to revoked offers.
- Abroad-path problems (fake agents, loan burden) were not researched in this pass.
- Direct Reddit and Quora phrasing could not be retrieved. Quotes are limited to Careers360 question titles.
