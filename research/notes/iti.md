# ITI / vocational trade students and parents: problems during and after training

Research date: 20 October 2026. Scope: India only. Method: web search and fetch, about 16 tool calls. Several primary PDFs (NITI Aayog report, DGT NIOS FAQ) returned 403 or unreadable binary, so some figures come from press summaries of those documents. This is flagged on each line. Problem-table tags use the app's feature names: Compare Paths, What If, Eligibility, Exam Stack, Cost Loan & ROI Calculator, Document Check, Foundation Check, Explore, My Plan, Parent Mode, or "new feature needed".

## Q1. After ITI: apprenticeship, lateral-entry diploma, railway, PSU/defence, foreign jobs, self-employment. What do students not know?

### Takeaway
An ITI pass-out has at least six routes: Act/NAPS apprenticeship, lateral entry into Diploma year 2, RRB ALP/Technician, Railway Act Apprentice, PSU apprenticeships, and overseas trade recruitment. Each route has its own eligibility rules, and those rules depend on trade, state, NCVT vs SCVT certification and marks. The information is spread across state notices and coaching sites. A student cannot easily see which routes are open to *their* trade and certificate.

### Cited Findings
- **Apprenticeship stipend floor.** Under the Apprenticeship (Amendment) Rules 2019, the minimum stipend is Rs 6,000/month for Class 10 pass-outs and Rs 7,000/month for "National or State Certificate holder" (this covers ITI). It rises 10% in year 2 and 15% in year 3 — [Vikaspedia](https://en.vikaspedia.in/viewcontent/social-welfare/skill-development/schemes-for-skill-development/apprenticeship-training); [Legitquest, Amendment Rules 2019](https://www.legitquest.com/act/apprenticeship-amendment-rules-2019/8D10). A September 2020 Lok Sabha answer said the government was not then considering an increase — [gconnect summary](https://gconnect.in/orders-in-brief/pay/revised-rate-stipend-apprentices.html). (I did not verify whether rates changed after 2020.)
- **NAPS employer incentive.** NAPS reimburses employers 25% of the stipend, capped at Rs 1,500 per apprentice per month, plus basic-training cost up to Rs 7,500 for fresh apprentices. Employers must register on the apprenticeship portal — [Tamil Nadu NAPS document](https://skilltraining.tn.gov.in/ATS-PDFs/NAPS_Document_Process.pdf); [2COMS NAPS benefits](https://trainatwork.2coms.com/images/pdf/NAPS_Benefits.pdf). (These describe the older NAPS design. NAPS-2 changes were not verified here.)
- **Advertised apprentice stipends are low.** One pharma apprenticeship for ITI-Fitter in Vizag advertised Rs 12,000/month with food and transport. Delhi-NCR fresher Electrician/Fitter listings start around Rs 12,000–15,000/month — [WorkIndia listings](https://www.workindia.in/iti-fresher-jobs-in-badli-indus.-area-delhi/). These are advertisements, not verified pay data.
- **Lateral entry rules differ by state:**
  - Nagaland admits 10th + 2-year ITI to Diploma year 2 on academic merit, capped at 10% of approved intake — [Nagaland DTE](https://dte.nagaland.gov.in/index.php/admission-in-polytechnics/).
  - Tamil Nadu notices require the ITI to be in an "appropriate" trade — [PSG Polytechnic 2024](https://www.psgpolytech.ac.in/adm2024/ADMISSION%20INTO%20DIPLOMA%20PROGRAMMES.pdf).
  - An older (2015) Andhra Pradesh G.O. required 60% at ITI plus bridge subjects not covered in ITI — [AP lateral entry G.O. 2015, OLDER](https://itiwebbasedonlineexamination.yolasite.com/resources/bridge/Polytechnic%20Lateral%20Entry%20G.O.%2C%20Guidelines%2C%20Syllabus%2012.05.15%20(1).pdf).
  - AP also runs a current lateral-entry notification for 2026–27 — [APSCTE Lateral entry 26-27](https://apdhte.nic.in/apscte/LateralEntry_Jan_June_26/Lateral_entry_26-27.pdf).
  - Some states use an entrance test (for example AP's ECET/"VOCLET"-type routes, as described in Q&A answers) — [Careers360 Q&A](https://www.careers360.com/question-after-iti-copa-to-diploma-college).
- **RRB ALP 2025 (CEN 01/2025, about 9,970 posts).** Eligibility is Matriculation + ITI (NCVT/SCVT) in listed trades, OR Matriculation + Act Apprenticeship in those trades, OR a 3-year engineering diploma. Candidates awaiting results were reported ineligible.
  - Sources disagree on the age limit (18–30 vs 18–33) — [Oliveboard](https://www.oliveboard.in/blog/rrb-alp-qualification-2025/); [SSCAdda trade list](https://www.sscadda.com/rrb-alp-trade-list/); [Careers360](https://competition.careers360.com/articles/rrb-alp-2025).
  - All three are aggregators. The official CEN wording was not fetched.
- **SCVT is valid for Railway Act Apprentice (2026).** A March 2026 Western Railway corrigendum for Act Apprentices says SCVT-issued ITI certificates "shall also be considered valid", in addition to NCVT — [RRC-WR corrigendum](https://www.rrc-wr.com/rrwc/Files/2692.pdf). The fact that a corrigendum was needed suggests applicants were confused or excluded.
- **Railway Board order RBE 147/2014** mapped ITI Centre-of-Excellence qualifications to CTS trades for railway employment — [staffnews.in, 2015, OLDER](https://www.staffnews.in/2015/01/recognition-of-certificatequalification.html).
- **PSU apprenticeships.** Q&A answers point electronics ITI holders to PSU apprenticeships (ONGC, BPCL, NPCIL) and describe PSU apprenticeships as about 1 year against about 6 months in the private sector — [Careers360 Q&A](https://www.careers360.com/question-hi-what-education-can-i-pursue-after-iti-in-electronic). These are user-forum answers, not verified durations.
- **Foreign jobs (Israel).** In January 2024 Israel ran skill tests at ITI Lucknow and in Rohtak for construction trades (carpenters, iron workers, tile fitters, plasterers). One candidate cited a salary of Rs 1.37 lakh/month. Applicants said low wages and no jobs at home pushed them — [Scroll](https://scroll.in/article/1063082/low-wages-high-unemployment-whats-pushing-labourers-to-take-up-construction-work-in-israel); [The Wire](https://thewire.in/labour/would-rather-stay-here-but-there-are-no-jobs-say-those-at-israel-recruitment-drive-in-haryana); [Al Jazeera, Jan 2024](https://aljazeera.com/features/2024/1/24/undeterred-by-gaza-war-thousands-of-indians-turn-up-for-jobs-in-israel). Trade unions opposed the drive (details not retrieved).
- **Self-employment.** NITI Aayog concluded that most trainees are neither employable nor skilled enough to start their own enterprises — [nationalskillsnetwork summary of NITI report](https://www.nationalskillsnetwork.in/important-recommendations-to-transform-industrial-training-institutes-itis-niti-aayog-report/).

### Inferences
- **Problem A1 (completed): "Which routes are open to me, for my trade?"** Eligibility depends on the trade, NCVT/SCVT, 1-year vs 2-year trade, ITI marks and state.
  - Evidence: state lateral-entry notices and the RRB ALP trade list above.
  - Solvable: **yes**.
  - Feature: **Eligibility**, made ITI-aware: trade × certificate type × duration × state → list of open routes, each linked to its source notice and a "check current notice" warning.
- **Problem A2 (completed): Apprenticeship vs Diploma lateral entry vs job/RRB prep.** These are three different time and money trade-offs.
  - Solvable: **yes**.
  - Feature: **Compare Paths** with ITI-specific rows: months to first income, stipend floor (Rs 7,000 + 10%/15%), what certificate you end with (NAC / Diploma / job), and which later doors each opens (ALP accepts Act Apprenticeship; diploma opens B.Tech lateral entry).
- **Problem A3 (completed): Students do not know Act Apprenticeship is itself an RRB ALP qualifying route,** or that the SCVT/NCVT wording varies by notice.
  - Solvable: **partly**.
  - Feature: **Exam Stack** for railway/PSU technician exams, restricted to trade-matching posts. Needs dated, sourced notice data.
- **Problem A4 (completed): Overseas trade jobs carry high pay with high risk** (war zone, recruiter fraud).
  - Solvable: **partly**.
  - Feature: **new feature needed**, an "Overseas trade job check". It would explain government routes (NSDC / state skill corporations, MEA eMigrate) against private agents, and the visa/ECR basics. It must not rank or endorse agents.
- **Problem A5 (completed): Self-employment readiness.**
  - Solvable: **partly**.
  - Feature: **Cost Loan & ROI Calculator** in a "set up a workshop" variant (tools capital, scheme loans). Scheme names and terms were not researched here (gap).

### Gaps
- NAPS-2 (2023 onward) DBT stipend share and current minimum stipend rates were not verified from primary sources.
- Official RRB ALP CEN 01/2025 text and RRB Technician / RRC Group D ITI rules were not fetched. Group D accepts Class 10 *or* ITI *or* NAC per common reporting, but this was not verified.
- Defence technician routes (for example Army/Navy/Air Force ITI-based tradesman entries, ordnance) and PSU technician recruitment norms were not researched.
- No apprenticeship completion or absorption rates were found.

## Q2. Certificates and institutions: NCVT vs SCVT, delayed or non-issued certificates, fake or de-affiliated ITIs, trade-choice regret, NIOS/CBSE equivalence

### Takeaway
Industry and many recruiters value NCVT over SCVT. Certificate issuance has a long history of delays and portal failures. DGT is actively de-affiliating ITIs in 2026, and their enrolled trainees are left in limbo. The new NIOS–DGT scheme (2026) lets ITI trainees earn Class 10/12 by studying only 1–2 NIOS subjects. This matters directly for students who later need "12th pass" eligibility. Many will not know it exists.

### Cited Findings
- **NCVT vs SCVT.** NITI Aayog found industry values NCVT certificates more than SCVT — [Careers360 on NITI report, Feb 2023](https://news.careers360.com/niti-aayog-iti-study-placement-admission-students-teachers-vocational-education).
- **Court case on NTC (Kerala, 2023).** The Kerala High Court (KSEB Meter Reader rank list, 2023) reportedly held that including candidates without a National Trade Certificate was not legal and ordered the list rearranged — [Kerala Kaumudi, Nov 2023](https://keralakaumudi.com/web-news/en/2023/11/NMAN0452490/1.html). The page now returns 410, so this is search-snippet only. Verify on Indian Kanoon before citing in product.
- **Court case on ITI Electrician certificates (Bombay HC).** Petitioners (Kritikakumari v. State of Maharashtra) challenged the rejection of ITI Electrician certificates, citing circulars that equate ITI with COE courses — [caseon.in](https://www.caseon.in/case/kritikakumari-vs-the-state-of-maharashtra). The outcome was not confirmed.
- **Unrecognised certificate (Punjab & Haryana HC).** A headline reports the court held that employment secured via an unrecognised certificate is void ab initio even after 6 years of service — [lawyerenews](https://lawyerenews.com/legal_detail/service-law-employment-secured-via-unrecognized-certificate-void-ab-initio-6-years-of-service-creates-no-equity-punjab-haryana-high-court). The details, and whether it concerned an ITI, were not confirmed.
- **Delayed certificates (official, OLDER).** The Labour Ministry admitted in Parliament that there is "some times" a delay in issuing certificates, because states take considerable time to declare results and send data. It forwarded 21,51,647 pending certificates (Oct 2012–Oct 2014) to State Directors and launched NCVT-MIS e-certificates for admissions from August 2014 — [Lok Sabha answer, 2014–15, OLDER](https://eparlib.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/669038/1/23010.pdf); [Lok Sabha answer, OLDER](https://eparlib.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/702394/1/5335.pdf).
- **Portal failures:**
  - The NCVT MIS results site crashed or reported "too many users" at result time — [Republic World](https://www.republicworld.com/education/ncvt-mis-iti-result-website-not-working-candidates-face-difficulty).
  - In 2023, mark sheet/certificate downloads were disabled until 17 September 2023 due to CTS exam activities — [uLektz news, Aug 2023](https://news.ulektz.com/2023/08/14/ncvt-mis-iti-2023-result-released-heres-how-to-download-marksheets/amp/).
- **De-affiliation in 2026.** DGT orders state that trainees already enrolled in de-affiliated ITIs must be allowed to complete training, with no fresh admissions. In one Rajasthan-linked case, trainee data "shall be reallocated" to other affiliated institutes. 661 grievances came in from affected ITIs by 30.05.2026, with GRC decisions published July–August 2026 — [DGT 31st SCAA de-affiliation order, Rajasthan, Jul 2026](https://www.dgt.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-07/De-affiliation%20order%20in%20respect%20of%2031st%20SCAA%20MoM%20-Rajasthan%20State%20ITIs_0.pdf); [DGT GRC minutes, Aug 2026](https://dgt.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-08/GRC%20MOM%20531.pdf); [DGT 31st SCAA MoM](https://www.dgt.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-07/31st%20SCAA%20MoM__0.pdf).
- **Fake certificates:**
  - In Bengaluru, a couple running an ITI were arrested for issuing fake marks cards and degree certificates to about 500 students — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Fkarnataka%2Fbengaluru%2Fin-guise-of-running-iti-couple-issues-fake-marks-cards-to-500-college-dropouts-1019601.html) (date not confirmed, likely OLDER).
  - A report alleges fake ITI certificates were issued for government jobs from North Kashmir ITIs for payment — [Daily Excelsior](https://www.dailyexcelsior.com/?p=1026) (undated, OLDER).
- **NIOS–DGT academic equivalence scheme (DGT, 2026):**
  - Credits: 2-year NTC trainees get 4 subjects credited via Transfer of Credit and study only 1 language from NIOS. 1-year NTC trainees get 3 credited and study 2.
  - At least one Group A language is mandatory.
  - Entry level: after Class 8 → NIOS Class 10; after Class 10 → NIOS Class 12. A 1-year trade seeking Class 12 needs a 2-year gap after Class 10.
  - DGT says the equivalence is recognised by universities, departments and employers.
  - Sources: [DGT NIOS_DGT_scheme page](https://dgt.gov.in/en/CTS/NIOS_DGT_scheme); [DGT FAQ PDF, May 2026](https://dgt.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-07/FAQ-NIOS-DGT-scheme.pdf); [DGT brochure](https://dgt.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-07/Brochure-NIOS-DGT-scheme.pdf). The FAQ PDF was not machine-readable here, so details come from the search summary of the DGT page.
  - Accreditation norms for ITIs as NIOS study centres — [NIOS](https://nios.ac.in/media/documents/accr/ITIs%20ACC.%20NORMS_ACC%20APP_CP_AFFIDAVIT_MOU_TUTOR_CHECKLIST_APP%20FORM%20FOR%20LEARNER_MERGED.pdf).
- **CBSE.** No CBSE notification on ITI equivalence was found in this search.
- **Trade choice and admissions.** NITI recommended admission based on aptitude checks rather than only Class 8/10 marks, plus a JoSAA-like central admission portal. This implies trade allocation today is not aptitude-led — [Careers360 on NITI](https://news.careers360.com/niti-aayog-iti-study-placement-admission-students-teachers-vocational-education).

### Inferences
- **Problem B1 (before or studying): "Is this ITI real, affiliated, NCVT or SCVT, and what is its grade?"** 12,352 ITIs are graded, with an average rating of 1.41 (see Q3). De-affiliations are happening in 2026.
  - Solvable: **yes**.
  - Feature: **new feature needed**, an "ITI Check". The student enters the ITI name or code and gets a link to DGT/NCVT-MIS affiliation and grading lookups, a de-affiliation-list warning, and an NCVT vs SCVT explanation. The app must link to official lookups rather than certify anything.
- **Problem B2 (studying, at a de-affiliated ITI): "Will I still get my certificate?"**
  - Solvable: **partly**.
  - Feature: **Document Check** plus a guide that explains the DGT rule (enrolled trainees complete; data may be reallocated) and the grievance / State Directorate contact route.
- **Problem B3 (completed): Certificate or mark sheet not issued or not downloadable.**
  - Solvable: **partly**.
  - Feature: **Document Check**, an ITI version with a checklist covering the NCVT MIS e-certificate, roll number, mark sheet and the State Directorate escalation path. **My Plan** can hold a reminder to download it before job applications.
- **Problem B4 (completed): "SCVT certificate rejected for a job."**
  - Solvable: **partly**.
  - Feature: **Eligibility** should flag per-notice NCVT/SCVT wording (for example the WR 2026 corrigendum). It must never claim universal acceptance.
- **Problem B5 (studying/completed): Needs "12th pass" for a later job or degree.**
  - Solvable: **yes**.
  - Feature: **What If**: "What if I take the NIOS–DGT route?" This shows which doors open once Class 10/12 equivalence is added. **My Plan** step: NIOS enrolment window.
- **Problem B6 (before admission): Trade-choice regret** (for example being allotted a trade by marks).
  - Solvable: **partly**.
  - Feature: **Explore** ITI trades with day-to-day work, the post-ITI routes each trade unlocks (ALP-eligible trades, lateral-entry branch mapping), and women-friendly or non-traditional trades.

### Gaps
- Verified Indian Kanoon citations for SCVT/NTC recognition cases were not obtained.
- No 2024–26 news on large-scale certificate delays was found. Current scale is unknown.
- The NIOS–DGT FAQ fees and exam calendar were not extracted.
- No CBSE equivalence notice was found.
- No data was found on how often trainees change trade or drop out.

## Q3. Stigma, low pay, placement quality, women, official report findings (NITI Aayog 2023, DGT)

### Takeaway
The NITI Aayog report (January/February 2023) describes these problems:
- **Placement:** extremely low tracked placement (about 0.09% recorded).
- **Seats:** about 48% seat utilisation.
- **Instructors:** only 36% of sanctioned posts filled.
- **Women:** women 6.6% of admissions in 2021.

DGT disputed some of these facts. Women's share in ITIs is reported anywhere from about 6.6% to 20%, depending on source and year.

### Cited Findings
All NITI figures below are via [Careers360, 10 Feb 2023](https://news.careers360.com/niti-aayog-iti-study-placement-admission-students-teachers-vocational-education). The primary PDF [niti.gov.in ITI report](https://niti.gov.in/sites/default/files/2023-02/ITI_Report_02022023_0.pdf) returned 403.

- **Placement:** 405 placed out of 4,14,247 trained (0.09%).
  - Tamil Nadu 3.2% (248/7,676).
  - Bihar 11 placed of 1,18,162 who passed in 2020.
  - Haryana 2 of 28,423.
  - Placement cells are often staffed by faculty without relevant expertise.
  - Caveat: these are *tracked* placements, which likely under-count real employment.
- **Institutions:** 14,789 DGT-affiliated ITIs, 78.40% private. Seat utilisation is 43.07% private, 56.74% government, 48.20% overall, out of 25,38,487 seats.
- **Instructors:** 36% of sanctioned posts filled. 15% of over 95,000 instructors trained per guidelines.
- **Grading:** 12,352 ITIs graded, average rating 1.41. Only 990 (8%) qualified for STRIVE funding.
- **Cost:** about Rs 1.32 lakh per student.
- **Fees:** private ITIs collect partial fees, then chase students for the balance.
- **Women:** 6.6% of admissions in 2021. Barriers are lack of hostels, transport and non-traditional trades.
- **Study basis:** interviews with 40+ principals, 100 instructors and 1,000+ trainees.
- **Government pushback.** A parliamentary committee report says DGT found "factual inaccuracies and incorrect presumption(s)" in the NITI report. The committee urged a placement and entrepreneurship cell in each ITI plus MSME tie-ups — [Lok Sabha Standing Committee report 58](https://eparlib.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/2975915/1/17_Labour_Textiles_and_Skill_Development_58.pdf).
- **Women, conflicting figures:**
  - DGT's 2023 state-workshop slides show female ITI enrolment at 12.22% (2019), about one-tenth of enrolment — [DGT PPT](https://dgt.gov.in/sites/default/files/2023-12/StateworkshopPPT.pdf).
  - The Wire says women are under 15% of enrolment, clustered in trades like Sewing Technology — [The Wire](https://thewire.in/labour/india-skill-development-crisis-itis-reform). Its headline reports "Near 50% Seats in ITIs Stay Vacant". The body was not retrievable.
  - Another report says women's share rose from 12% (2017-18) to 20% (2022-23) — reported via search summary, source among [eGov/Elets Dec 2024](https://egov.eletsonline.com/2024/12/ministry-of-skill-development-empowers-itis-through-strive-initiative/). The exact source is unconfirmed.
  - The 36.59% women figure covers all MSDE schemes, not ITIs — [Rajya Sabha answer, 24 Jul 2024](https://rsdebate.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/749139/1/PQ_265_24072024_S29_p92_p93.pdf).
- **Trainee dissatisfaction.** An older survey reported via iMOVE Germany found unhappiness over inadequate stipend, few opportunities to learn new skills, low-end jobs, and ad-hoc/contract trainers — [iMOVE, undated/OLDER](https://www.imove-germany.de/de/alle_news/13606.htm).
- **Low pay at entry.** Fresher ITI Electrician/Fitter adverts in Delhi-NCR start around Rs 12,000/month — [WorkIndia](https://www.workindia.in/iti-fresher-jobs-in-badli-indus.-area-delhi/).
- **Trainer quality commentary** — [PolicyCircle](https://policycircle.org/opinion/iti-reform-must-start-with-trainers/); [Drishti IAS on the Sep 2024 grading report](https://www.drishtiias.com/daily-updates/daily-news-analysis/grading-report-highlights-itis-performance/print_manually).

### Inferences
- **Problem C1 (before admission, parents): "Is a private ITI worth the fee?"** Private ITIs have lower seat use, low average grades and fee-chasing.
  - Solvable: **yes**.
  - Feature: **Cost Loan & ROI Calculator**, ITI version: total fees + months + stipend income, set against advertised entry pay ranges, shown as ranges with sources. **Parent Mode** shows the same view.
- **Problem C2 (completed): Weak placement cell; student must find work alone.**
  - Solvable: **partly**.
  - Feature: **My Plan** checklist covering apprenticeshipindia.gov.in registration, NCVT MIS certificate, and Rozgar/state job portals. The app is not a job board and must not imply placement.
- **Problem C3 (studying, women): Hostel, transport and safety; concentration in traditional trades.**
  - Solvable: **partly**.
  - Feature: **Explore** filter for women-only ITIs / National Skill Training Institutes for women and non-traditional trades. **Parent Mode** content on safety and hostel questions. This needs verified directory data (gap).
- **Problem C4 (all): Social stigma ("ITI is for failures").**
  - Solvable: **partly**.
  - Feature: **Compare Paths** showing ITI → Act Apprenticeship → ALP or ITI → Diploma → B.Tech ladders, factually, with no guaranteed outcomes. **Parent Mode** explainer. No direct stigma survey evidence was found (see gaps).

### Gaps
- No primary-source survey on ITI stigma, parental attitudes or trainee regret (2020–26) was found.
- No PLFS-based ITI earnings/unemployment figures were retrieved.
- The DGT Annual Report 2024–25 women's share was not retrieved.
- The NITI report PDF could not be read directly.

### Inferences
- The questions map directly to **Eligibility** (low marks, NCVT), **Compare Paths** (job vs diploma vs apprenticeship) and **Explore** (trade → government job).

### Gaps
- No Reddit, YouTube-comment or Hindi/Odia-language forum data was gathered. Vernacular phrasing is likely dominant and is not captured.
- No frequency data exists on which questions are most common.

## Q5. Feature fit: which current features do NOT fit ITI students, and ITI-specific replacements

### Takeaway
Most features carry over only if they become trade-aware and certificate-aware. Two need replacing: entrance-exam-centric Exam Stack and an academic Foundation Check. Two features are missing: an ITI/institution check and an apprenticeship tracker.

### Cited Findings
These rest on the evidence above:
- Trade/state-dependent eligibility — [Nagaland DTE](https://dte.nagaland.gov.in/index.php/admission-in-polytechnics/); [Oliveboard ALP](https://www.oliveboard.in/blog/rrb-alp-qualification-2025/)
- Stipend floors — [Vikaspedia](https://en.vikaspedia.in/viewcontent/social-welfare/skill-development/schemes-for-skill-development/apprenticeship-training)
- De-affiliation — [DGT order](https://www.dgt.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-07/De-affiliation%20order%20in%20respect%20of%2031st%20SCAA%20MoM%20-Rajasthan%20State%20ITIs_0.pdf)
- NIOS–DGT — [DGT](https://dgt.gov.in/en/CTS/NIOS_DGT_scheme)

### Inferences
**Features that do not fit as they stand:**
- **Exam Stack.** Built for JEE/NEET/CUET-style entrance stacks. ITI students mostly face a few trade-matched recruitment exams (RRB ALP/Technician, RRC Group D, PSU technician) and state lateral-entry tests.
  - Replacement: **"Trade Job Exam Stack"**, filtered by trade + NCVT/SCVT + age. It should show trade-matching rules and Act-Apprentice routes.
- **Foundation Check.** An academic-subject readiness check does not fit trade skills.
  - Replacement: **"Trade Skill & Bridge Check"**. It covers bridge subjects needed for lateral-entry diploma (maths/science gaps per state notice) and NIOS–DGT language-subject readiness. It is not a certified aptitude test.
- **Cost Loan & ROI Calculator.** Built around college fees and education loans, which do not fit.
  - Replacement: an **"Earn-while-learning" calculator** covering private ITI fees, apprenticeship stipend (Rs 7,000 floor + 10%/15% increments, to be verified for current rates), relocation cost, and payback of overseas-job agent fees. Figures shown as sourced ranges.

**Features that fit with an ITI variant:**
- **Compare Paths** — Apprenticeship vs Lateral-Entry Diploma vs Railway prep vs Overseas vs Self-employment.
- **What If** — "What if I add NIOS Class 12?" / "What if my certificate is SCVT?" / "What if I do Act Apprenticeship first?"
- **Eligibility** — trade × NCVT/SCVT × trade duration × state × marks.
- **Document Check** — NCVT MIS e-certificate, mark sheet, apprenticeship contract and NAC, eMigrate/ECR for overseas jobs.
- **Explore** — trades, women-friendly institutes, ITI routes per trade.
- **My Plan** — apprenticeship registration, certificate download, NIOS enrolment, notice dates.
- **Parent Mode** — fee worth, stigma, safety for daughters, overseas-job risk.

**New features needed:**
1. **ITI Check.** Look up an institute and get links to DGT affiliation, grading and de-affiliation lists. Information only, no certification.
2. **Apprenticeship Tracker.** Covers portal registration, contract, stipend-floor check, and the NAC exam step.
3. **Overseas Trade Job Safety Check.** Covers government recruitment routes against agents, and ECR/eMigrate basics.
4. **Certificate Problem Guide.** Who to contact when an NCVT MIS certificate is missing or wrong.
