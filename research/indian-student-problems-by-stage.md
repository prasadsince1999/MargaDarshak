# Show each stage the gates it faces

Across all seven stages, the problem Mārgadarshak is best placed to solve is the same one: **a hidden gate that nobody showed the student in time**. Examples include a subject that closes an exam, a certificate that expired before counselling, a portal button that cannot be undone, a fee deadline missed by minutes, a diploma that a recruiter does not treat as Class 12, a NET score that expires after a year, or a second JEE Advanced attempt that has to fall in the very next year. These are rule-and-date problems. Court records show students losing seats and jobs to them, and the Supreme Court has had to use Article 142, its discretionary power, to rescue some of them ([Bar & Bench](https://barandbench.com/news/supreme-court-comes-aid-dalit-student-couldnt-pay-iit-admission-fee); [LiveLaw](https://livelaw.in/top-stories/iit-bombay-supreme-court-student-admission-wrong-click-167019)). An app with dated, source-linked rules can catch them early. The emotional problems (parental pressure, prestige, burnout) and the structural ones (graduate unemployment, ITI placement, fellowship delays, exam leaks) are real and often larger, but an app can only change the information the family argues from and keep a backup route visible. The evidence also says the app's current features are built for one student: a Class 10 or Class 12 CBSE student heading for JEE/NEET. For Diploma, ITI, Bachelor's, Master's and dropper users, most features have to be rebuilt around different inputs. A diploma student needs branch, semester aggregate, domicile and recognition. An ITI student needs trade, NCVT/SCVT and duration. A graduate needs CGPA, backlogs, age and attempts. A Master's student needs NET category, marks floor and score validity. Stream comparison and the JEE/NEET Exam Stack should be hidden for everyone past Class 11. Ten new features have enough evidence behind them. The strongest four are a cross-document identity and certificate-validity check, a counselling-step explainer with fee deadlines, an institution/programme recognition lookup that links to official lists, and a "doors open / doors closed" map at Class 10. Many of the rules in this report reached the notes through coaching sites or news, so they must be checked against the primary bulletin before going into the app. Section 9 lists the ones that need checking first.

## 1. How to read the evidence labels in this report

Every problem below carries one of six evidence labels. **Official rule/data** means a government, regulator, board or portal document, or official statistics. Where these reached us through a news or coaching summary rather than the primary PDF, the label says **"via secondary"**. **Court judgment** means a reported order of the Supreme Court, a High Court or a tribunal. **News report** means mainstream or education-news journalism. **Survey** means a structured sample study, with its sample noted. **Single anecdote** means one forum question, one school or one person, mostly Careers360 Q&A titles, because Reddit and Quora could not be reached in this research pass. **Coaching-site claim** means a coaching institute or commercial education blog, which often sells the product being discussed.

"How often" is ranked by how many independent sources show the problem and "how severe" by the harm documented. No India-wide dataset counts how common any of these problems is, so the rankings are judgements from the evidence pattern, not measured frequencies.

*Added 21 October 2026:* government survey figures for Odisha and All-India now give scale to several stages: coaching, Class 10 dropout and transition, unemployment by qualification, youth not studying or working, and course spend. See [MoSPI data note](../data/survey-figures/mospi-2026-10-21.md). They describe groups, not how often any specific gate problem happens.

The student questions under each stage are close paraphrases of real Careers360 question titles; the cited link leads to the original wording. Where a rule is shown, the app must show its source and a "last checked" date and say "confirm in this year's notice". Where a check is a self-check, the app must say "checked by you", never "verified".

## 2. Class 10: parents decide, but nobody shows which doors close

**The student's real question:** "Which stream should I take after 10th, and if I take Science, can I still go to Commerce or Arts later?" ([Careers360](https://www.careers360.com/question-if-i-take-the-science-stream-after-class-10-then-am-i-eligible-for-all-the-fields-of-humanities-and-commerce-if-i-want-to-take-them-in-future-1/amp)). A sibling's version: "Our parents are forcing him into PCM when he wants PCB to become a doctor. Should he take PCMB?" ([Careers360](https://www.careers360.com/question-my-brother-is-in-class-10-2021-22-giving-board-exams-afterwards-he-wants-to-take-science-stream-pcb-he-wants-to-become-a-doctor-but-our-parents-are-forcing-him-to-take-pcm-and-forcing-him-to-do-enginnering-what-should-he-do-or-should-he-take-pcmb)). In Odisha the question is often simply when +2 admission opens and closes ([Careers360](https://www.careers360.com/question-is-2-addmission-open-and-when-are-addmission-close)).

The defining fact at this stage is an information vacuum among the families Mārgadarshak targets. In the UNICEF/iDreamCareer survey of **4,968 mostly low-income Class 9–12 students across 25 states, only 9.36% had received career guidance or knew it existed**, and family was the top influence on career decisions at 30% ([The Week / PTI](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/national/2024/07/18/del88-unicef-education-report.html)). A better-off sample (IC3/FLAME) found 68% with counselling access ([FLAME](https://flame.edu.in/in-the-media/over-80-students-benefit-from-career-counselling-40-lack-access-report)). The gap between the two is the reason a free app aimed at state-board and Odisha families fills a real hole. The parent is the main decision-maker here, not an observer, so Parent Mode matters more at Class 10 than anywhere else.

The checkable harms are one-directional. Science students can usually move to Commerce or Arts later, but the reverse is mostly closed ([Indo American Public School](https://indoamericanpublicschool.com/?p=3474)). NMC told the Supreme Court that NEET needs Physics, Chemistry, Biology and English at 10+2, with Biology later allowed as an additional subject ([LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/top-stories/neet-candidates-studied-class-11-12-physics-chemistry-biology-english-eligible-nmc-supreme-court-191594); [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/education/news/students-clearing-10-2-without-biology-still-eligible-to-appear-for-neet-123112300195_1.html)). CBSE's Basic-vs-Standard Maths rule for Class 11 has been relaxed every year since COVID, through 2025–26, on an "ability and aptitude" condition, and **no source was found for 2026–27** ([Careers360 News](https://news.careers360.com/cbse-class-10-students-basic-math-allowed-study-mathematics-in-11th-for-2025-26-board-exams-loc-subjects/amp)). One Mumbai school saw **47 of about 290 students ask to change stream within three months** of Class 11. That is a single school, reported by a counselling provider, and must not be shown as a national rate ([Dheya](https://www.dheya.com/insights/how-340-students-in-mumbai-found-their-right-stream-before-the-10th-board-results-came-dheya-insights-dheya-career-mento)).

Odisha's +2 admission system (SAMS) adds its own mechanics: a common application form (CAF), merit lists, slide-up requests, spot rounds and short reporting windows ([Careers360 School](https://school.careers360.com/articles/sams-odisha-plus-two-admission-2025)). Even so, **over 2 lakh +2 seats stayed vacant** after two rounds in 2025 ([New Indian Express via Magzter](https://www.magzter.com/stories/newspaper/The-New-Indian-Express-Bhubaneswar/OVER-2L-PLUS-II-SEATS-VACANT-SPOT-ADMISSION-FROM-TODAY)).

## 3. +2 (Class 11 → Class 12 → awaiting results → counselling): unfixable clicks and expired certificates

**The student's real questions** change as the two years go on. In Class 11: "Can I change my stream after CBSE registration?" ([Careers360](https://www.careers360.com/question-can-i-change-my-class-11th-stream-after-cbse-registration)). In Class 12: "Is the 75% criterion for NIT waived or not?" ([Careers360](https://www.careers360.com/question-is-75-percent-criteria-for-nit-is-waived-off-or-not)). Then: "I swapped my addresses on the JEE form and missed the correction window. Will it hurt counselling?" ([Careers360](https://www.careers360.com/question-i-filled-oppposite-present-and-permanent-address-in-jee-form-and-also-lost-the-correction-chance-what-can-i-do-now-is-it-going-to-imapct-me-during-admission-or-counselling)). After CUET: "Can I add a subject I never studied to my DU preference list?" ([Careers360](https://www.careers360.com/question-i-gave-cuet-exam-this-year-now-i-have-to-make-preference-list-for-delhi-university-i-want-to-keep-my-preferences-in-history-political-science-and-sankrit-but-i-did-not-have-sanskrit-in-class-xii-and-would-not-be-able-to-do-subject-mapping-of-it-so-can-i-add-sanskrit-in-my-preference-list)).

This stage has the strongest court evidence in the whole research set, and it all points at small procedural failures with irreversible results. **Siddhant Batra (JEE Advanced rank 270) clicked "withdraw" instead of "freeze" on JoSAA and lost his IIT Bombay seat.** The Bombay HC dismissed his petition, and the Supreme Court restored the seat in December 2020 ([LiveLaw](https://livelaw.in/top-stories/iit-bombay-supreme-court-student-admission-wrong-click-167019)). **Atul Kumar, from a BPL family, arranged the ₹17,500 acceptance fee at 4:45 PM against a 5 PM deadline and still lost the seat.** The Supreme Court used Article 142 to create a supernumerary seat at IIT Dhanbad in September 2024 ([Bar & Bench](https://barandbench.com/news/supreme-court-comes-aid-dalit-student-couldnt-pay-iit-admission-fee)). Prince Jaibir Singh got the same discretionary relief at IIT Bombay ([Scroll](https://scroll.in/latest/1011191/sc-asks-iit-bombay-to-create-seat-for-dalit-student-who-could-not-pay-his-fee-on-time)). The rescues were expressly not precedent, and High Courts often refuse. The Karnataka HC would not reopen a NEET-UG 2026 payment portal for a candidate who had waited until the last date ([Lawyer E News](https://lawyerenews.com/legal_detail/neet-ug-2026-karnataka-high-court-refuses-to-reopen-payment-portal-for-candidate-who-waited-till-last-date-to-pay-fees)). The app's message must therefore be "don't rely on the court"; the realistic prevention is knowing the fee and the deadline in advance.

Certificates are the second trap. In 2026 the Calcutta HC let a WBJEE candidate with an expired EWS certificate into counselling only on condition he produced a current one, or he would be treated as General ([LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/high-court/calcutta-high-court/calcutta-high-court-allows-candidate-to-participate-in-wbjee-counselling-despite-expired-ews-certificate-subject-to-renewal-542763)). The same court refused NEET candidates who wanted to switch to EWS after the merit list because they held no certificate when they applied ([CourtBook](https://courtbook.in/posts/neet-ug-2026-calcutta-hc-rejects-plea-to-change-category-to-ews-after-merit-list)). Form errors are just as final. In JEE Main 2025, students who had entered their domicile state instead of their Class 12 board state as "state of eligibility", or whose name did not match Aadhaar, had no correction route ([ALLEN news](https://news.allen.in/jee-main-2025-students-troubled-following-no-correction-in-name-state-of-eligibility/), coaching-site news).

The third cluster is fraud and recognition. A Lucknow fake consultancy bought NEET-qualified students' data, and one family paid ₹45 lakh ([Careers360 News](https://news.careers360.com/mbbs-admission-scam-two-held-lucknow-rs-1-crore-fraud-over-fake-seats-neet-qualified-students-engineering-hind-medical-college)). The ED reported about 18,000 NEET seats filled using forged NRI certificates ([Careers360 News](https://news.careers360.com/mbbs-admission-scam-exposed-18000-neet-ug-pg-seats-filled-using-forged-nri-quota-certificates-ed-reports-mcc-indian-embassy/amp)). UGC keeps an official fake-university list ([UGC](https://www.ugc.gov.in/universitydetails/Fakeuniversity)). Over all of this sits exam-system instability: NEET-UG 2026 was cancelled and re-held on 21 June after leak allegations, affecting about 22 lakh candidates ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/education/exams/story/neet-ug-2026-re-exam-to-take-place-on-june-21-over-22-lakh-students-affected-531620-2026-05-15)). Kota recorded 26 coaching-student suicides in 2023 and 17 in 2024 by news counts ([Deccan Herald](https://deccanherald.com/india/rajasthan/neet-aspirant-hangs-self-in-kota-12th-student-suicide-since-january-3083551)).

## 4. Diploma / Polytechnic: four parallel routes, and a Class 12 equivalence that can vanish at verification

**The student's real questions:** "Can I sit JEE Main after a three-year diploma?" ([Careers360](https://www.careers360.com/question-can-i-attend-jee-mains-exam-after-3years-diploma)); "Which is better after a diploma, lateral-entry B.Tech or the regular four-year B.Tech?" ([Careers360](https://www.careers360.com/question-what-is-better-after-diploma-lateral-entry-btech-or-4-yr-btech)); "Can the 2023–26 diploma batch fill the RRB JE form?" ([Careers360](https://www.careers360.com/question-2023-2026-batch-diploma-can-fill-the-form-rrb-je-2025/amp)). The questions are short, low-English and eligibility-first. The best answer is a Yes / No / Depends-on-X card that names the governing document.

The final-year diploma student faces a **parallel-deadline problem**. The routes are a state lateral-entry test (OJEE LE-Tech, AP/TS ECET, KEA DCET, DTU LEET), central and state JE exams, PSU trainee drives and NATS apprenticeship, each with its own percentage cutoff, domicile rule and "must have passed vs final-year allowed" rule. Odisha's LE-Tech used 45% (40% SC/ST) in 2024 ([Careers360 News](https://news.careers360.com/ojee-2024-counselling-begins-for-lateral-entry-courses-at-ojeenicin-eligibility)), while a private Odisha university sets 60% for its own LE seats ([SOA](https://www.admission.soa.ac.in/lateral-entry-2026)), so the app must say which body's rule it is showing. AP ECET requires AP local status ([APECET 2025 booklet](https://cets.apsche.ap.gov.in/ECET/PDF/APECET2025_InstructionBooklet.pdf)). In 2020 AICTE had to issue a circular because institutions were refusing diploma holders from "other" branches ([Careers360 News](https://news.careers360.com/allow-diploma-students-lateral-entry-btech-aicte)).

The highest-harm failure is **late rejection on equivalence**. In *Harishankar Upadhyay v. Union of India* (Delhi HC, 2023), diploma holders were shortlisted for an RRB post requiring "+2 or equivalent", called to document verification and then rejected; the petition was dismissed ([Indian Kanoon](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/25991594/)). The Supreme Court has held that courts cannot declare equivalence; the recruiting authority decides ([Unnikrishnan, Indian Kanoon](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/131862371/); [Devender Bhaskar, SC PDF](https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2007/13481/13481_2007_7_1501_31460_Judgement_24-Nov-2021.pdf)). Regulators disagree with one another: the Bar Council told the Madras HC that a 3-year diploma counts as Class 12 for LLB ([LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/news-updates/madras-high-court-3-year-diploma-course-will-be-treated-on-par-with-12th-certificate-for-admission-to-llb-course-210535)). The app must never say "diploma = 12th"; it must say "accepted only where the notification or a government order says so".

The system itself is under strain. AICTE data shows polytechnic vacancy of **33–48% between 2017-18 and 2022-23**, 59% of polytechnics are private, and faculty shortages are acknowledged ([Careers360 News, AICTE data](https://news.careers360.com/polytechnic-courses-diploma-engineering-colleges-crisis-aicte-autonomy-vacant-seats-teacher-low-poly-admission-jobs-10th-12th)). One government polytechnic's "64% placed" figure includes students who went on to higher studies (same source), so placement percentages need labels.

## 5. ITI: six routes, a certificate type that changes everything, and institutes being de-affiliated in 2026

**The student's real questions:** "I've passed ITI in Fitter. What can I do next?" The existing evidence library has **zero ITI cards**, so everything here is new. An ITI pass-out has at least six routes: Act/NAPS apprenticeship, lateral entry into diploma year 2, RRB ALP/Technician, Railway Act Apprentice, PSU apprenticeships and overseas trade recruitment. Each turns on trade, NCVT vs SCVT certification, 1-year vs 2-year duration, marks and state. The certificate type matters in practice: a March 2026 Western Railway corrigendum had to clarify that **SCVT certificates "shall also be considered valid"** for Act Apprentice posts ([RRC-WR](https://www.rrc-wr.com/rrwc/Files/2692.pdf)), which suggests applicants had been confused or excluded. RRB ALP 2025 accepts Matriculation + ITI, Matriculation + Act Apprenticeship, or a diploma, but aggregators disagree on the age limit (30 vs 33) ([Oliveboard](https://www.oliveboard.in/blog/rrb-alp-qualification-2025/)). That figure must be checked against the official notice before it goes into the app.

The official picture of the system is grim but disputed. NITI Aayog's 2023 study, reported because the primary PDF returned 403, found **405 tracked placements out of 4,14,247 trained (0.09%)**, 48% seat utilisation, only 36% of instructor posts filled, an average institute grade of 1.41 among 12,352 graded ITIs, and women at 6.6% of 2021 admissions ([Careers360, Feb 2023](https://news.careers360.com/niti-aayog-iti-study-placement-admission-students-teachers-vocational-education)). DGT told a parliamentary committee the report had "factual inaccuracies" ([Lok Sabha Standing Committee report 58](https://eparlib.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/2975915/1/17_Labour_Textiles_and_Skill_Development_58.pdf)). The placement figure counts tracked placements only, so it is a measure of how badly placement is recorded, not proof that 99.9% are jobless. In July–August 2026, DGT orders de-affiliated ITIs. Enrolled trainees may finish and may have their records moved to other institutes, and **661 grievances** had come in by May 2026 ([DGT order](https://www.dgt.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-07/De-affiliation%20order%20in%20respect%20of%2031st%20SCAA%20MoM%20-Rajasthan%20State%20ITIs_0.pdf); [DGT GRC minutes](https://dgt.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-08/GRC%20MOM%20531.pdf)).

The most underused opportunity is the **NIOS–DGT scheme**: ITI trainees can earn NIOS Class 10 or 12 by studying only one or two NIOS subjects, with trade subjects credited. DGT says universities and employers recognise the result ([DGT](https://dgt.gov.in/en/CTS/NIOS_DGT_scheme); [DGT brochure 2026](https://dgt.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-07/Brochure-NIOS-DGT-scheme.pdf)). This directly answers the "12th pass required" wall.

The official JEE Advanced page allows **at most two attempts in consecutive years**, limits the Class XII year, and makes anyone admitted to an IIT through JoSAA 2025 ineligible ([JEE Advanced](https://jeeadv.ac.in/eligibility.html)). That makes a pause year a permanent loss of the IIT route. A drop year costs roughly ₹1.5 lakh in coaching plus ₹50,000 for a hostel, according to a Lok Sabha question of unclear date ([eparlib](https://eparlib.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/2966444/1/12882.pdf)).

The mental-health evidence is serious, and it is mostly not dropper-specific. NCRB recorded **13,892 student suicides in 2023, the highest in a decade** ([Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/ncrb-report-2023-over-13800-student-suicide-deaths-highest-in-10-years-national-crime-records-bureau-maharashtra-up-tn/amp)). In *Sukdeb Saha* (2025 INSC 893), about a 17-year-old NEET aspirant's death, the Supreme Court issued 15 interim guidelines covering coaching centres, including counsellors for institutions with 100+ students and avoiding performance-based batch segregation ([SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2025/07/25/supreme-court-guidelines-protecting-mental-health-of-coaching-and-college-students-legal-news/)). The 2024 MoE coaching guidelines bar misleading promises and enrolment under 16 ([MoE PDF](https://education.gov.in/sites/upload_files/mhrd/files/document-reports/Guideliens_Coaching_Centres_en.pdf)). A much larger and quieter group is the board-exam failures: **about 50 lakh failed Class 10/12 in 2024**, only about 13 lakh were in NIOS, and the Centre plans to track them through UDISE and route them to NIOS from 2026 ([Republic World](https://www.republicworld.com/education/centre-to-track-failed-class-10-12-students-from-2026-offer-counselling-open-schooling-route); [DD India](https://ddindia.co.in/2025/12/centre-to-track-failed-class-10-12-students-from-2026-link-them-with-nios-for-re-admission/)). ## 10. The existing evidence library: fix the sources, then fill four gaps

The 86-card library ([evidence library](https://ksmxtech.com/margadarshak/evidence-library/)) is honest at the page level: every card says "Review pending". The underlying sources have specific defects that should be fixed before any card is promoted. **Three cards have no source link at all**: #47 (the Gujarat "Teli"/SEBC NEET seat case), #48 (Aryan Shende, Bombay HC, lost caste certificate) and #54 (Sunanda, OBC e-dossier). #7's Quora URL appears truncated, and its card text says "diploma" while the quote describes civil engineering. **One YouTube URL backs three different stories** (#36, #40, #41); at most one can be accurate. Other shared URLs are #20/#21, #61/#63 and #8/#69, and #63's SC/DU story does not obviously match its linked question. Six platform labels are wrong: #16 (court, links to news), #30 (news, links to Reddit), #55 (LinkedIn, links to a blog), #56 (Twitter, links to a wiki), #75 (court, links to Scribd), #76 (news, links to a wiki). #85 and #86 are identity-document cases filed under Foundation. Re-tagging them gives Document Check 25 cards and leaves Foundation Check with four. About 24 cards name real or apparently real people, against the research guide's own rule. Some situation texts add unsourced numbers ("24 deaths in 8 months"; "9 of 1,200"). The companion 86-stories document calls the set "Verified" and "Canonical", misnumbers several cards, and states an "April 1 rule" and "85–90% syllabus overlap" that the research guide explicitly rejects. It should be used only as an index.

This research gives replacement or reinforcing primary sources for several weak spots. On certificates, it adds Supreme Court and High Court cases with Indian Kanoon, LiveLaw or court-PDF links: Calcutta HC on EWS, Delhi HC on the OBC-NCL window, Jigya Yadav. On counselling, it adds three Supreme Court cases where the library had none. On equivalence and recognition, it adds the Harishankar, Unnikrishnan and Devender Bhaskar judgments and the UGC lists. On droppers, it adds the official JEE Advanced eligibility page and the NEET seat-qualifier data, so the library no longer depends on YouTube testimony.

Four gaps are now partly filled. ITI and diploma, both zero in the library, now have official notices and court cases. Master's has fellowship, NET and recognition evidence. Scholarships, almost absent, now have CAG audits, the Punjab and Telangana delays and the INSPIRE order. Class 10 has the UNICEF survey and the SAMS mechanics.

Real gaps remain. **No first-person Reddit, Quora, YouTube-comment, Hindi or Odia student voice was gathered for any stage**: every search failed to reach Reddit, so "real questions" rest on Careers360 Q&A titles, many dated 2018–2022. **Odisha-specific evidence is still thin**. It is limited to SAMS 2025, OJEE schedules, LE-Tech cutoffs and the NIOS–OSEPA partnership. Odisha ITI/SCVT practice, CHSE subject rules, Odisha polytechnic admission, the state scholarship portal and Odisha SET status were not researched, even though Odisha is the app's directory focus. No national data measures how often any of these problems happens: no counts of seats lost to counselling errors, students switching stream, backlog exclusions or fellowship delays. Several primary bulletins were never opened (NTA JEE/NEET/CUET/NET, CBSE bye-laws, SAMS prospectus, NITI PDFs). Topics still uncovered include girls' mobility and early-marriage barriers, disability accommodations, ragging and UG campus safety, abroad-study agents and loans, UPSC/PSC attempt and age rules, MBA/MCA/MCom student phrasing, and NSP or Odisha-portal court cases. Missing case names also need retrieving: the Delhi HC Coast Guard order, the MP HC Aadhaar-age ruling and the *Mohit Kumar* citation.

## Conclusion

The evidence moves Mārgadarshak's centre of gravity. The library was built around stories of students who chose the wrong stream or college. The courts tell a different story: students who chose correctly and still lost the seat to a click, a fee window, an expired certificate or an equivalence clause. That makes the honest product less of an adviser and more of a **dated rulebook that speaks first**. This fits the studio's deterministic-engine principle (same inputs, same answer, source on screen) better than any recommendation feature, because each failure here has a date, a document and a governing body that can be named. The judgments also show that rescue is discretionary and rare, so the app's value is preventive, and that only works if it reaches students months before verification desks do.

The second implication is architectural rather than editorial. Past Class 11, every stage reasons from inputs the app does not yet hold: branch, trade, certificate type, CGPA and backlogs, NET category, attempt history. Adding stages by copying the Class 12 tool set would reproduce the "JEE/NEET-only" bias the library already shows. The cheapest high-leverage move is a shared rule-card format (rule, body, source, last-checked, applies-to-stage) that Eligibility, Exam Stack, Document Check and My Plan all read. Every new feature in the ranked list, from the equivalence flag to the NET unlock panel, then becomes a set of rule cards rather than a new engine. Building that format, then filling it first for Odisha, is where the evidence and the founder's base meet.
