# Color System

## Strategy

Margadarshak uses a warm Bauhaus Neo-Brutalist palette. The product should feel like ink printed on paper: high contrast, direct, structured, and supportive. The old rounded blue guidance palette is not the current app language.

## Core Palette

- Ink: #1A1A1A
- Paper: #F5F0E8
- Paper Bright: #FAF7F2
- Paper Low: #EEE9E0
- Paper Dim: #D6D1C9
- Border Primary: #1A1A1A
- Border Muted: #D0CBC3
- Text Primary: #1A1A1A
- Text Secondary: #4A4A4A
- Text Inverse: #FFFFFF

## Accent Palette

- Accent Yellow: #FFCC00
- Accent Red: #E63B2E
- Accent Blue: #0055FF

Yellow is the default primary action fill. Red is reserved for warning, urgency, destructive action, blocked states, or emotional emphasis. Blue is reserved for links, information, and roadmap/path highlights.

## Semantic Usage

- Background: paper
- Standard surface: paper
- Raised surface: paperBright
- Muted surface: paperLow
- Primary action: yellow fill with ink text
- Warning: yellow fill with ink text
- Error/destructive: red fill with inverse text
- Success: green fill with inverse text
- Info/path: blue fill with inverse text

Accent colors are not automatically safe text colors. Normal text must target at least 4.5:1 contrast. Large text, icons, and borders must target at least 3:1 contrast.

## Surface Rules

- Do use warm paper backgrounds instead of pure white.
- Do use near-black borders for product structure.
- Do use flat accent blocks for hierarchy.
- Do not use soft pastel cards as the default product language.
- Do not use random gradients.
- Do not use glassmorphism, blur surfaces, or cyber/HUD effects as the base app language.

## Status Rules

- Eligible/success: green plus icon and text.
- Check/warning: yellow plus icon and text.
- Not eligible/error: red plus icon and text.
- Needs verification/info: blue plus icon and text.

Meaning must never rely on color alone.
