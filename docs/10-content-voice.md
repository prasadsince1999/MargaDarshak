# Content and Voice

## Voice principles
- Calm
- Simple
- Respectful
- Practical
- Honest
- Non-shaming

## Student copy rules
- Avoid fear-based language
- Avoid overpromising success
- Give next steps, not pressure

## Parent copy rules
- Explain field awareness clearly
- Respect concerns about safety, time, and career outcomes
- Avoid insulting traditional views

## Preferred phrases
- Your next step
- Check eligibility
- Explore backup paths
- Good fit for these interests
- Needs verification
- You can still choose another route

## Avoid
- Guaranteed success
- Best career for everyone
- One perfect path
- Follow your passion only
