# Mārgadarshak

A career decision app for Indian students and their parents: it shows the gates ahead of a student at their stage, with sources and dates, so they can choose a path and keep a real backup.

## Language

### Where the student is

**Stage**:
The education tile a student picks in onboarding: Class 10, +2, Diploma, ITI / Vocational, Bachelor's, Master's, or Dropper / Gap.
_Avoid_: Level, class number, education level

**Status**:
Whether the student is studying, awaiting a result, or has completed their stage.
_Avoid_: Progress, state

**Qualification**:
Something the student has completed, such as Class 10, Class 12, ITI (NCVT or SCVT), Diploma, Bachelor's or Master's, with its board or branch and year.
_Avoid_: Class level, education

**Finish year**:
The calendar year the student expects to complete their current course or board exam.
_Avoid_: Current year, year of study, semester

**Not studying now**:
A student who has stopped at any stage — to retake an exam, to take a break, or after leaving a course — and is not enrolled in a course today. Shown to students as "Dropper / Gap".
_Avoid_: Failure, gap year (as a label for all cases)

**Decision moment**:
The real decision a student faces next, worked out from their stage, status and qualifications (A to G).
_Avoid_: Persona, segment

### What the student wants

**Career category**:
A broad, practical area of work a student can aim for, such as Defence, Medical, Engineering & IT, Civil Services or Own business.
_Avoid_: Field, sector, goal

**Role**:
A specific job inside a category, such as Army officer or Doctor.
_Avoid_: Career (when a specific job is meant), post, dream job

**Route**:
A specific way into a role — an exam, course or recruitment — with its own eligibility and dates, such as NDA, CDS or MBBS.
_Avoid_: Path (for a single exam), option, goal

**Government group**:
The official class of a government post — Group A, B, C or D (Level 1) — taken from its recruitment notice and shown on the role, never on a career category.
_Avoid_: Class (alone), grade, rank

**Government level**:
Whether a government post belongs to the Central Government or a State Government; state posts are shown for the student's own state first.
_Avoid_: Sector, public sector (it suggests PSUs)

**Dream**:
What a student (or a parent, as a Parent's pick) chooses to aim for: one focus category with up to three roles, plus at most one backup category.
_Avoid_: Goal, aspiration, target (in student-facing text)

**Self-discovery check**:
A short activity that suggests the three career categories that best fit a student's interests and strengths, with reasons; it helps exploration and is not a test score.
_Avoid_: Aptitude test, assessment result, IQ

**Yearly check-in**:
A warning-style card on Home each June, and when the finish year passes, asking whether the student is still studying, has finished or changed course. It comes back on every app open until answered; until then the student's facts stay as last confirmed.
_Avoid_: Profile update reminder

### What reaches the student

**Update**:
A reviewed, sourced notice sent to a student about something they asked to follow, such as an exam date change, admit card, result or new recruitment.
_Avoid_: Alert, news, notification (as a product term)

**Rule card**:
One rule from an official document, stored with its source, section, cycle and the date it was checked.
_Avoid_: Fact, policy, guideline

**Reviewer**:
A KSM × Tech team member who checks every drafted update against its official source before any student sees it.
_Avoid_: Admin, moderator, counselor

**Counselor**:
A vetted person who talks with a student (and their family) using the app's counselor tools, paid only by KSM × Tech, never by institutions, and seeing a profile only with consent.
_Avoid_: Advisor, mentor, reviewer

**Parent's pick**:
A dream or interest a parent chose for their child, always shown with that label and never ranked above the student's own choices.
_Avoid_: Parent goal, parent preference
