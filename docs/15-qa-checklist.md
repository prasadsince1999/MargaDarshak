# UI QA Checklist

## Clarity
- Does the screen have one main purpose?
- Is the primary action obvious?
- Does the user know what to do next?

## Consistency
- Are colors from tokens only?
- Are spacings from the spacing scale?
- Are radii consistent?
- Are text styles using approved roles?

## Accessibility
- Are touch targets large enough?
- Is text readable?
- Is contrast good in light and dark modes?
- Is meaning shown with text/icon, not color only?

## Product fit
- Does the screen feel calm and trustworthy?
- Does it help a confused student or parent quickly?
- Is official information clearly separate from AI suggestions?

## Content quality
- Is the wording simple?
- Is there unnecessary jargon?
- Are warnings helpful and not fear-based?

## Final check
- Would a student from a small town understand this?
- Would a parent understand the main message in under 10 seconds?
