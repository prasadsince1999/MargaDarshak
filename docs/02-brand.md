# Margadarshak — Brand & Voice

> Last updated: June 2026

---

## Brand Name

**Margadarshak** — a guide who shows the path.

## Brand Role

A decision system for Indian students and parents to explore career paths, backup routes, and document readiness before confusion turns into regret.

---

## Brand Philosophy

Every competitor says: *"We will guide you."*

Margadarshak says: **"Here is your map. You decide."**

That distinction — **map vs. counselor, student agency vs. expert dependency** — is the entire brand identity. Every post, every bio, every headline should feel like it came from that philosophy.

> *"All the what-if? questions, answered before the decision becomes regret."*

---

## Competitor Landscape

| Competitor | What they do | Their language |
|---|---|---|
| **margdarshak.org** | NGO, trains career advisors, runs CAP workshops | Service, Advisors, Network, Programmes |
| **margdarshakedu.com** | College admission + counseling services | Counseling, Expert guidance, Career planning |
| **margdarshak.co.in** | Career aptitude testing + psychometric tools | Assessment, Report, Psychometric, Expert |
| **margdarshan.tech** | Job/internship + upskilling platform | Opportunities, Skill, Placement, Hire |
| **margadarshak.in** | Stream/career exploration tool | Explore, Options, Profile, Interests |
| **maargdarshak.com** | Coaching center locator, admission queries | Coaching, Admissions, Counselors |

**The pattern:** They all frame the student as someone who needs a *human expert or counselor*. They use words like counseling, assessment, advisor, expert, network, and sessions. They position *themselves* as the guide.

**Our product does the opposite.** Margadarshak puts the map in the student's hand — no counselor needed, no expert session, no appointment. The student and parent navigate together using the app.

| Word | Why |
|---|---|
| **Path map / Road map** | The app builds a visible map, not advice |
| **What-if** | Signature feature, no competitor has this |

| **Family conversation table** | Deeply Indian, no one else uses this |
| **Clarity, not comfort** | Honest, not motivational |

## Words to Never Use

Every competitor already owns these:

| Avoid | Why |
|---|---|
| ~~Free~~ | Devalues the product; breaks trust if subscription comes later |
| ~~Trust-first~~ | Sounds like self-praise; nobody asked |
| ~~High-stakes~~ | Too heavy, hard to understand |
| ~~Guidance-first~~ | Sounds like a counseling service |
| ~~Counseling / Counselor~~ | Positions us as expert-dependent |
| ~~Guidance expert / Advisor~~ | We are the map, not the guide |
| ~~Career assessment / Report~~ | Sounds like psychometric testing |
| ~~Sessions / Appointments~~ | We are on-demand, not appointment-based |
| ~~Platform / Portal~~ | Generic tech language |
| ~~Empowerment~~ | Vague startup word |
| ~~Ecosystem~~ | Too abstract |
| ~~We guide students~~ | Positions us as the counselor |
| ~~Navigating~~ | Overused in tech |
| ~~Privilege~~ | Can sound preachy |

## Voice Principles

| Principle | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Calm | No panic, no urgency tricks |
| Simple | No jargon, no academic language |
| Respectful | No judgment on choices or background |
| Practical | Real next steps, not motivational quotes |
| Honest | Risks and trade-offs stated clearly |
| Non-shaming | Never makes average students feel inferior |

---

## Student Copy Rules

- Avoid fear-based language.
- Avoid overpromising success.
- Give next steps, not pressure.
- Use "your" not "our experts will".

## Parent Copy Rules

- Explain field awareness clearly.
- Respect concerns about safety, time, and career outcomes.
- Avoid insulting traditional views.
- Lead with data, not emotion.

## Voice Formula

Every post should sound like:

- **Clarity over hype**
- **Systems over slogans**
- **Real situations over generic motivation**
- **Maps over advice**
- **Evidence over noise**

**Good:** "A student can prepare for one exam and still miss all backup options just because no one showed the full map."

**Bad:** "We are transforming career guidance in India."

### Post Framing Rules
- App updates should say what changed *for the student*, not just "feature added."
- Student stories should end with visibility/design/better systems, not sadness.
- Parent posts should lead with data, not emotion.
- Never make the page sound like a sad-problem page. Always move toward clarity, structure, and progress.
